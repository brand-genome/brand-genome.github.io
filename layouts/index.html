What is Brand Genome?

The Brand Genome is a first-of-its-kind Knowledge Graph that maps the complete visual and textual DNA of the world's largest companies. It breaks brand identity down into three computable layers:

The Genotype (The Building Blocks): The granular, trackable attributes of every piece of media—lighting, posture, colors, and tone.

The Phenotype (The Expression): How those building blocks come together in tweets, advertisements, and public messaging over time.

The Environment (The Market): How a brand's unique genetic makeup positions it against competitors and drives real-world consumer engagement.
