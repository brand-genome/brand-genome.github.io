## Brand Guidelines

### 2017

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Working with Nest is about anticipating people’s needs and making their lives easier, not just linking to devices and controlling them remotely [^1].

##### Core Values

- simplicity
- clarity
- anticipation of needs
- safety
- consistency

##### Target Audience

- Developers and marketers creating products and communications that integrate with Nest smart home devices, aiming to enhance user experience in connected homes [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- clean
- minimalistic
- professional

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses clean, modern, and illustrative visuals with geometric shapes, minimal lines, and a focus on simplicity. Approved color palettes include Nest Blue, Nest Gray, and specific accent colors. Typography is clear and modern, and logo usage is strictly regulated for clarity and consistency. Icons and product imagery are minimalistic and must follow strict guidelines for size, color, and clear space [^3].
