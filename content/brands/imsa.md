## Brand Guidelines

### 2023

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To maintain a consistent and recognizable visual identity for IMSA, unifying messaging across all IMSA spaces and establishing clear communication recognizable by IMSA's partners and its audience [^1].

##### Core Values

- consistency
- clarity
- recognizability
- integrity
- creativity

##### Target Audience

- IMSA partners, audience, employees, competitors, race fans, and stakeholders [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- dynamic
- professional
- modern
- energetic
- sporty

##### Visual Identity Overview

- IMSA's visual identity centers on a bold red, white, and black color palette, accented by gradient graphics and geometric shapes. The logo always appears on white, supplemented by a 'track' graphic device. Typography is clean and modern, using Aktiv Grotesk and Arial. Visual style emphasizes speed, motion, and clarity, with consistent use across all brand materials, signage, uniforms, and communications [^3].
