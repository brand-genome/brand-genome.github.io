## Brand Guidelines

### 2021

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The Arts Council is the national agency for funding, developing and promoting the arts in Ireland [^1].

##### Core Values

- funding
- development
- promotion
- consistency
- legibility

##### Target Audience

- Artists, arts organizations, and stakeholders in Ireland's arts sector [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- professional
- expressive
- versatile
- modern
- minimalistic

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a carefully crafted logo, a defined color palette, and the Patron typeface. The logo is used consistently across print and digital media, with clear rules for sizing, positioning, and color usage. The style is clean, modern, and minimalistic, emphasizing legibility and balance [^3].

#### Categories

##### Logo Usage

- The Arts Council logo has been created for use in print and on-screen. The characters have been carefully crafted to ensure balance, legibility and clear reproduction.Therefore, these characters may not be redrawn or rearranged in any way as to do so would affect the aesthetic of the mark.
- For legibility reasons, the logo should not be printed or displayed on screen below the recommended height. Print 15mm Screen 60px The following is a guide to proportional sizing according to various print formats: DL 15mm A5 15mm A4 21mm A3 30mm A2 42mm A1 60mm A0 80mm
- The Arts Council Logo should be positioned in the top left, bottom left or bottom right corners of a printed design layout. The logo should be positioned at least the distance of the clearspace from the edge of the page.
- The Arts Council Logo should be positioned in the top left, bottom left or bottom right corners or the middle centre or middle bottom of a video layout. The logo should be positioned at least the distance of the clearspace from the edge of the frame.
- When using the Arts Council logo in conjunction with other marks, ensure that it has the same visual weighting, is optically aligned and adheres to the clearspace.
- The logo should never be rendered smaller than any other mark placed in the same field of vision.
- It is important that the logo is used correctly and consistently across all applications.The logo should not be altered in any way that will weaken its legibility or render it unrecognisable and should only ever be reproduced from the master artwork supplied.
- Do not alter the logo or its characters in any way. (Fig A)
- Do not outline the logo. (Fig B)
- Do not stretch or distort the logo. (Fig C)
- Do not reconstruct or improvise the logo. (Fig D)
- When placed on imagery, the Arts Council logo should appear in black against a light background image, or in white against a dark background image.
- The black logo should be used on white or light coloured backgrounds. The white logo should be used on black or dark coloured backgrounds.
- The logo can also be represented in colour. This colour should come from one of the main swatches used in a particular piece.
- Colour contrast should be considered to ensure there are no problems with legibility.

##### Typography

- Patron is the chosen typeface for The Arts Council and must be used on all external communications. It is an expressive, yet versatile grotesk that is legible in print, web and mobile interfaces.
- It is available to download at www.milieugrotesque.com
