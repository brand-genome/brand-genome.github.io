## Brand Guidelines

### 2020

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To promote pride and unity in Indonesian-made products, emphasizing love and support for local craftsmanship and national identity.

##### Core Values

- pride
- unity
- support for local products
- national identity

##### Target Audience

- Indonesians who value local products and national pride, including youth, families, and brand partners seeking to highlight Indonesian craftsmanship.

##### Personality Traits

- vibrant
- energetic
- affectionate
- patriotic
- modern

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a stylized red heart with white arms or hands, radiating lines for emphasis, and bold, curved uppercase text. The color palette is primarily red and white, with modern sans-serif typography and clear logo usage rules. The overall style is simple, bold, and energetic, conveying pride and affection for Indonesian products.
