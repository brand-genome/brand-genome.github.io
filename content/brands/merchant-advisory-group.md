## Brand Guidelines

### 2024

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To strengthen the MAG’s reputation and prominence in the industry by building a consistent brand identity that assists the audience in recognizing MAG across all platforms and contributes to the broader goal of strengthening the global merchant voice and providing payments education opportunities.

##### Core Values

- consistency
- clarity
- collaboration
- education
- industry leadership

##### Target Audience

- Payments industry professionals, merchant members, conference attendees, sponsors, and partners involved in payments processing, fraud, authentication, and related topics.

##### Personality Traits

- professional
- modern
- clear
- strong
- collaborative

##### Visual Identity Overview

- MAG’s visual identity is clean, modern, and professional, emphasizing clarity and strength. The brand uses a strong geometric sans-serif logo in dark blue, a consistent color palette dominated by blue with accent colors, and minimalistic layouts with ample white space. Typography is standardized with Calibri and Arial, and visual elements are structured for legibility and brand recognition.
