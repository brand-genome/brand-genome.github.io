## Brand Guidelines

### 2016

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Leading to Better.

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a modern, clean logotype with a dark blue and orange color palette. The logo is used consistently across communications, with strict guidelines for clearance, minimum size, and approved color variations. The brand emphasizes clarity, contrast, and professional presentation, with visual toolkit instructions for logo usage and prohibited modifications. The tagline 'Leading to Better' is recommended for external communications and appears with the logo on cover pages. There are also approved variations for single-color and Chinese logotype applications, maintaining a minimalistic and professional look throughout all brand materials.

#### Categories

##### Brand Imagery

- Person with Food and Mesh Barrier - The image should depict a person holding a piece of food with their right hand, with their fingers grasping it, and their left hand also holding a piece of food, with visible fingers and thumb. The person's face is partially visible at the top, showing only the area around the mouth and chin, with long, brown, slightly wavy hair flowing down past the shoulders. The person is wearing a dark-colored, long-sleeved shirt or jacket. In front of the person's face, there should be a fine, transparent, and dotted mesh or string stretched horizontally across the image, with evenly spaced tiny white dots, creating a semi-transparent barrier. The background behind the person is blurred, highlighting the hands, food, and mesh as the main focus. The overall scene has a neutral to slightly dark lighting, emphasizing the hands, food, and mesh pattern.
- Illuminated Beaded Fashion - The image should feature a human figure, possibly a woman, dressed in a black or dark-colored jacket or coat, standing against a dark background. The person is adorned with a decorative accessory or garment made of numerous small, white, and blue glowing or illuminated beads or dots arranged in a flowing, wave-like pattern that covers the upper body and shoulder area, creating an effect of movement and light. The pattern is dense in some areas and gradually disperses, giving a dynamic, sparkling appearance. The person's posture is upright, with the head not visible in the frame. The lighting emphasizes the beads or dots, making them appear luminous, and casting a subtle glow on the surrounding fabric. The overall mood of the image is elegant and futuristic, highlighting the contrast between darkness and the illuminated decorative elements.
