## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Connect4Climate is a global partnership program launched by the World Bank Group and the Italian Ministry of Environment, Land and Sea together with the German Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development, that takes on climate change by promoting solutions and empowering people to act. It aims to raise awareness for climate action, promote commitment among the public, private sector and policy-makers for a rapid transition to a low-carbon resilient future, and build coalitions to further advocacy efforts and support sustainable development [^1].

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is modern, clean, and vibrant, emphasizing green tones and environmental themes. It features a stylized globe and leaf motif, streamlined logo configurations, and consistent use of specific typefaces and color palettes. The brand assets include legacy and alternative marks, tree and earth motifs, and iconography for initiatives, all designed to reinforce the global, solutions-focused, and sustainable nature of the brand [^3].
