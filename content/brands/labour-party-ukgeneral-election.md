## Brand Guidelines

### 2024

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To achieve impactful and strong brand recognition for the Labour Party during the General Election, emphasizing clarity, consistency, and striking visuals.

##### Core Values

- clean
- striking
- consistent

##### Target Audience

- Voters and the general public across the United Kingdom, including specific regional audiences in Scotland and Wales.

##### Personality Traits

- honest
- clear
- bold
- patriotic

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses a bold, geometric visual style with a primary color palette of Labour Red, Flag Blue, White, and Accessible Black. The logo features a stylized rose and is always left-aligned with breathing space. Typography is based on the Poppins geometric sans-serif typeface, emphasizing clarity and hierarchy. Visual elements include stylized representations of national flags and abstract geometric designs, ensuring consistency and high impact across all materials.
