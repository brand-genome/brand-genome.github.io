#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The Dinosaur logo is the primary identity for Dinosaur Bar-b-que. It unifies the restaurant across various locations and has come to symbolize what Dinosaur Bar-b-que stands for. The brand aims to create a bold, authentic, and gritty barbecue experience, emphasizing unity and recognition across all touchpoints [^1].

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a stylized dinosaur logo, distressed and bold typography, a palette of earthy and vibrant colors, and the use of rustic textures like wood, paper, and brick. The brand employs clear logo usage rules, strong color ratios, and a mix of weathered typefaces to evoke a vintage, gritty, and energetic barbecue vibe [^3].
