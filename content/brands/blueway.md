## Brand Guidelines

### 2016

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To provide a recreational water activity trail for non-motorised water activity enthusiasts, defined by accessible trail heads, egress points, and readily available trail information, promoting enjoyment of canals, rivers, lakes, and coastal areas, as well as associated land-based trails adjacent to water routes [^1].

##### Core Values

- accessibility
- recreation
- connection to nature
- community
- safety

##### Target Audience

- Non-motorised water activity enthusiasts, including kayakers, cyclists, walkers, and families seeking outdoor leisure and adventure [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- active
- welcoming
- modern
- tranquil
- professional

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses a strong, minimalist logo with wave motifs, a dominant blue and black color palette, clean sans-serif typography, and imagery reflecting outdoor recreation and natural landscapes. Visual elements include activity icons and wildlife illustrations, emphasizing clarity, accessibility, and a modern aesthetic [^3].
