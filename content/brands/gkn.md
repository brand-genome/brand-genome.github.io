## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To project a clear idea of what the company stands for, its aims, and how it differentiates itself from competitors, encapsulated in the phrase 'Engineering that moves the world.' GKN seeks to be easily identified and consistently represented across global markets, cultures, and countries [^1].

##### Core Values

- Consistency
- Coherence
- Market leadership
- Innovation
- Global recognition
- Authenticity
- Responsiveness
- Confidence
- Flexibility
- Imagination

##### Target Audience

- Stakeholders in more than 30 countries, including customers, partners, and employees in B2B markets where product differentiation is challenging and brand differentiation is key [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Straightforward
- Down-to-earth
- Clear
- Open
- Honest
- Factual
- Confident
- Flexible
- Imaginative
- Responsive

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is defined by consistent use of logos, corporate colours (GKN Silver Blue, GKN Yellow Gold, GKN Black, GKN Grey), quality and style of imagery, and design style in communications. Visual elements include the GKN logotype, ribbon, arrow, and a distinctive colour palette, applied across all brand touchpoints for clarity and impact [^3].

#### Categories

##### Brand Voice

- Though not visual, one of the most powerful ways we can communicate our identity and personality as an organisation is through the use of words. This applies to communications with all of our audiences -spoken, viewed or printed.
- It is essential that what we say is understood. That means using language that is straightforward, down-to-earth, and above all, clear. Be open and honest. Be factual and avoid exaggeration. Demonstrate confidence, flexibility, imagination and responsiveness.

##### Brand Imagery

- Our imagery reflects our brand personality. Our images are natural, down-to-earth, authentic and always clear and crisp.
- Our photography is inspirational, yet natural and grounded. We utilise realistic and relevant shots with ample natural lighting.
- Our photography becomes more dynamic by adding people or products in motion to our backgrounds.
- Using a shallow depth of field allows the viewer to focus on the main subject of an image. In addition, selective focus creates compelling moments with character, clarity and context.
- Introduce colour accents in our images that reflect our corporate colours of GKN Silver Blue and GKN Yellow Gold.

- The GKN symbol is the property of GKN plc. It may not be reproduced or otherwise used by non-GKN Group companies without the permission of GKN plc.
- The area indicated by the diagram below represents a minimum exclusion zone within which no other text or image is permitted.
- This creates an area of clear space around the logotype, away from other elements for clarity and impact.
- When reproduced in printed matter, the GKN symbol must never be reproduced smaller than 6mm high. On screen it must never be used smaller than 21 pixels high.
- The GKN logo is GKN Silver Blue and GKN Yellow Gold. See section 1.19 Colour palette for specifications. As a general rule, on printed matter, the GKN logotype sits on the top left hand side of the page.

- Never redraw, translate, or otherwise alter our logo in any way. The following list of incorrect use must be avoided to maintain the integrity of our brand.
- Do not condense
- Do not create sub-brands
- Do not expand
- Do not add shadows or effects
- Do not use other colour backgrounds
- Do not alter the typeface of the business descriptor
- Do not use on distracting photographic or patterned backgrounds
- Do not add site location
- The preferred application of the logotype is on a white background with the GKN symbol and business descriptor being produced in GKN Silver Blue and the chevron in GKN Yellow Gold.

##### Brand Values

- Our brand is what sets us apart in the markets and the more than 30 countries in which we operate. It’s about how we are perceived in the minds of our many different stakeholders, it defines what people expect from us and should reflect what we stand for. It is essential that the GKN brand can be easily identified across the many different markets, cultures and countries in which we operate.
- Brand identity refers to the visible elements that together identify and distinguish our brand. Different visual elements are used to represent the GKN brand and it is essential these are applied correctly and consistently to help support and strengthen our reputation. This includes consistent use of the logos and GKN corporate colours, the quality and style of imagery we use and the style of design of our communications.
- The fundamental idea behind a brand programme is that through everything it says and does a company can and should project a clear idea of what it stands for, what its aims are and how it seeks to differentiate itself from competitors who may produce very similar products and services at a very similar price.
- The most significant way in which this can be done is by articulating and presenting a consistency of purpose. So, corporate branding programmes seek to achieve consistency and coherence in appearances, communications and actions.
- In B2B markets where tangible product differentiation is frequently difficult to achieve, brand differentiation is commonly found not so much in 'what we do' as in 'how we do it'. The corporate brand is based around a central idea, a philosophy, a way of doing things. What the company stands for. What it believes in.
- For GKN this essence is encapsulated in the phrase. Engineering that moves the world.
- ‘Engineering that moves the world’ does not have to appear on all applications. It is not to be used on stationery or internal literature. It should be a driving force, a spirit, an essence that can be communicated both literally and laterally.
- Where the ‘Engineering that moves the world’ logotype appears, however, these guidelines should always be adhered to.
- The written phrase can also be used within text, where it is not in logo format, but should not include any arrow of any kind.
- GKN runs a number of operating companies, has various joint ventures and owns a number of brands. These operations and brands have great recognition in the marketplace -we embrace them, their values mirroring ours.

- Only the Directors, Company Secretary and those with specific delegated authority (for example, granted at a board meeting or by a power of attorney) have the legal authority to sign correspondence of a contractual nature on behalf of a company. - There is a difference between legal authority and financial authority. Individuals able to authorise payments up to specified amounts may not necessarily have the authority to sign contracts on behalf of the company if he or she is not an authorised signatory, even if the contract falls within that person’s authorised limits.
- Group Secretarial should be consulted if there is any doubt as to whether an employee has authority to sign a document that could legally bind the company.
