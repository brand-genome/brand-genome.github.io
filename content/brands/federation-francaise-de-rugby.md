## Brand Guidelines

### 2019

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To clarify its positioning, embrace its history, and look toward the future, the Fédération Française de Rugby aims to promote the growing diversity of rugby practices and unite all actors, collaborators, partners, and service providers under a new emblem, fostering authenticity, recognition, and preference for its brand [^1].

##### Core Values

- authenticity
- recognition
- attribution
- preference
- unity
- promotion of diversity in rugby practices

##### Target Audience

- All actors and enthusiasts of rugby, including experts and newcomers, female and male players, spectators, and players of all ages and levels; internal stakeholders such as employees, the federal network, and governing bodies; external stakeholders including the general public, media, and partners [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- strength
- conviction
- modernity
- clarity
- strategic vision

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a stylized red rooster emblem paired with exclusive typography and a color palette of blue and two shades of red. The logo is used in vertical, horizontal, and simplified forms, with strict rules for protection, proportions, and color usage. The design is clean, modern, and emblematic, emphasizing unity and recognition [^3].
