## Brand Guidelines

### 2023

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To protect the value and integrity of the OEKO-TEX® trademark, ensuring transparency, credibility, and consistency, and representing consumer protection, traceability, and transparency in textile and leather certification [^1].

##### Core Values

- consumer protection
- traceability
- transparency
- responsibility
- credibility
- integrity
- sustainability
- safety

##### Target Audience

- Businesses, manufacturers, brand owners, retailers, and all OEKO-TEX® partners who use OEKO-TEX® labels to mark certified products and communicate in the market [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- trustworthy
- responsible
- transparent
- credible
- consistent

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on the OEKO-TEX® logo, a teardrop-shaped green emblem with bold, white text, used across main and product brands. Labels feature consistent layouts, QR codes, and color palettes (OEKO-TEX® green, black, white), with strict guidelines for logo placement, minimum clear space, and approved color codes. Product labels are modular, emphasizing certification, traceability, and clarity [^3].
