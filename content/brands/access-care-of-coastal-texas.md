## Brand Guidelines

### 2022

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To build trust and reassurance with customers by ensuring all produced materials have a common style or theme, projecting a better image and saving time and money for the business [^1].

##### Core Values

- trust
- reassurance
- consistency
- efficiency
- professionalism

##### Target Audience

- Customers and designers working for Access Care of Coastal Texas, Inc., as well as business stakeholders seeking a unified brand presentation [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- consistent
- trustworthy
- professional
- reassuring

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses a stylized logo with versions for different backgrounds, a defined clearspace, Montserrat font for typography, a palette of primary and secondary colors, and consistent stationery design. Visuals emphasize gradients, clean layouts, and a modern, approachable style [^3].
