## Brand Guidelines

### 2017

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To provide modern, clean, and professional web design and digital marketing services, leveraging extensive experience to deliver relevant and effective branding solutions.

##### Core Values

- clean design
- professionalism
- modernity
- relevance
- collaboration

##### Target Audience

- Businesses and individuals seeking web design and digital marketing services, with a focus on technology and digital innovation.

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- professional
- clean
- innovative
- collaborative

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses bold, three-dimensional logos with layered, beveled edges, a monochromatic blue color palette, clean and modern typography (Industry family), and emphasizes high contrast and minimalism. Visuals often feature gradients, geometric shapes, and digital workspace themes, reinforcing a tech-oriented and professional aesthetic.

#### Categories

##### Brand Imagery

- The image should feature a prominently stylized, large, three-dimensional letter 'W' in the foreground, rendered with a glossy, modern appearance, and consisting of layered, beveled edges to create depth. The background should be a gradient blue, transitioning from a darker shade at the top to a lighter shade at the bottom, providing contrast for the 'W'. To the right of the 'W', there should be bold, white text that reads 'Next Theme', with 'Branding Guidelines' positioned underneath in smaller font size, also in white. The composition should be clean and professional, evoking a sense of modernity and branding focus, with the elements aligned to balance the visual weight between the large letter 'W' and the text on the right.
