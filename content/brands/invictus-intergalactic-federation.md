## Brand Guidelines

### 2022

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Invictus Intergalactic Federation was founded on strong military values, leveraging over 30 years of combined experience from its military members, and is focused around Star Citizen™ while constantly evolving with the space sim environment [^1].

##### Core Values

- military experience
- evolution
- knowledge
- skill
- consistency
- exploration
- boldness
- officiality

##### Target Audience

- Individuals and communities involved in Star Citizen™ and space simulation games, particularly those who value military structure and experience [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- bold
- official
- inspiring
- adventurous
- futuristic
- authoritative

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a circular emblem inspired by real-world military branches, featuring stylized rocket ships, stars, and clouds, with a monochromatic palette (navy blue, black, white) and clean, bold typography. Logos are used sparingly and with strict guidelines for scale, placement, and color to maintain consistency and officiality [^3].

##### Logo Usage

- This logo is to be placed on bright backgrounds. This is a official seal. Its to be used sparingly and for official cases/works only.
- Do not use the full color logo where the background clashes with the logo colors.
- We have 3 variations of our logo. The Navy Blue and Black logo are prefered to be used on brightly colored backgrounds.
- The white logo is to be used on dark backgrounds, that also includes our Navy Blue color.
- Although these are alternates, they are to be used sparingly. These two variations include the patch style, as well as the simplified version of the logo.
- Logo Lock-upWe have two logos that utilize our brand in a more formal and official capacity. These are to be used on official documents, letterheads, or to display/promote the organization in a positive light.The two variations include the stacked logo and the horizontal logo.
- We have a strict scale format that we require everyone to follow.
- The logo scales down in halves, down to a 1/8th. Please reference this scale for all further scales’ in this guide.
- The letters are also a good scaling tool and can be used for reference. The larger “P” is half the size of the logo and should only be used for main titles or partnership names.
- The smaller “p” is a quarter of the full logo size. This is to be used as sub-text, usually placed below the main title/text. The only exception is the full organization name.
- There must be a minimum space surrounding the stacked and horizontal logos.The bottom text is half the size of the logo, and the top is 1/4th. Combined, you must center them on the logo.
- For all partner lock-ups, it is required that you leave half a logo-width of space between all edges of the logo lock-up.
- The “2 logo width” is showing that 2 smaller logos should be able to fit within the main logo size. This is to keep consistency between all works. For more information about scale, please reference the page “Introducing our scale.”
- A simple horizontal logo should always be scaled to the vertical height of our logo.
- When using a simple logo, all elements (including small particles that may be apart of the logo) are to be scaled down to the vertical height of our logo.
- For horizontal logos and word-mark logos, the same guidelines apply. The logo must be scaled down to the vertical height of our logo.
- Stacked logos must also be scaled down to the vertical height of our logo so that the logos are the same size in thier entirety.
- To keep all things simple, you must scale down your logo, no matter the width and height, down to the vertical height of our logo.
- It is our preference that our white logo be used in conjunction with our partners logos on a dark or black background. If it’s to be used on a image, please reference the Logo | Partner lock-up scale page.
- It is encouraged that when using a white logo, the partners logo be white as well to have continuity throughout.
- The divider size shall not exceed 10px
- When displaying the logo along with our partners logos, it is acceptable to use their colors. Only display on bright backgrounds. That does not include images or video(s) unless approved.
- The divider should always be the color of the logo or black/white.
- Don’t alter or misuse the logo. You have better things to do.
- To keep consistency between all brands and partnerships, we do not allow alterations to the logo. This also applies to the partner logos when next to the INVFED logo.
- All posibilities are not listed and common sense should be used.
- Digital presentationWhen properly displaying the logo on digital work, the logo placement must be 2 smaller letters, or half the logo width. For more information on scale, reference our “Introducing our scale” page.
- The three prefered placements are top left, top right, and bottom right. If nessesary, it is acceptable to place the logo on the bottom left.It is our recomendation that when selecting a logo to use, consider the color of the logo. Do not allow the colors of the logo / word-mark to clash with the background of the image or work its being placed on.
