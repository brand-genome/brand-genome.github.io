## Brand Guidelines

### 2017

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- North Bridge House offers a co-educational, mixed ability independent education, challenging and inspiring pupils throughout every stage of their school career. The school aims for every pupil to achieve their full potential, academically and personally, through tailored approaches and specialist expertise at every stage.

##### Core Values

- Knowing the individual
- Academic excellence
- Nurturing character and wellbeing
- Happiness
- Professional expertise

##### Target Audience

- Families seeking independent, co-educational schooling in North London for children from Nursery through Sixth Form, including those looking for tailored support and academic excellence across all stages.

##### Personality Traits

- Friendly
- Supportive
- Professional
- Enriching
- Community-focused

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a shield crest with stylized initials, a clean sans-serif font, and a color palette dominated by navy blue, light blue, rich black, white, and accent colors. The brand uses professional, well-lit imagery of students in enriched environments, abstract student artwork, and consistent logo usage across stationery and signage.
