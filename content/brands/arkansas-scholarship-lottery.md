## Brand Guidelines

### 2019

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To fund college scholarships for Arkansas students and create winning people, winning futures, and winning moments through the Arkansas Scholarship Lottery [^1].

##### Core Values

- education
- opportunity
- optimism
- celebration
- community impact

##### Target Audience

- Arkansas residents, especially students and their families seeking scholarships, as well as lottery players and the broader community [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- fun
- playful
- modern
- approachable
- optimistic
- encouraging
- celebratory

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses bold, modern typography, bright and almost neon colors, posterized and energetic photography, and doodles to create a fun, graphic, and approachable style that differentiates it from traditional lottery brands [^3].

#### Categories

##### Brand Voice

- The fonts are bold and modern, adding to the fun and playful style of the brand.
- Showcasing the human face(s) of winning with the lottery stands at the forefront of the brand. The brand benefits most from showcasing real people's Point of Views (POVs) and real expressions in interesting, powerful and compelling ways that speak directly to individual priorities and viewpoints.
- The brand graphic is a contemporary mix of retro inspired script and condensed sans serif font designed to make the lottery appear fun, modern and approachable. The letters are slightly tilted upward, providing a subtle nod to the uplifting feeling of winning-whether winning money, getting a scholarship or enjoying the benefits that tax revenue provides. The larger exclamation point at the end drives home the excitement of game play.
- The bright, almost neon colors are attention-getting and fun, reinforcing the bold, graphic style of the brand.
- The photography is engaging, playful and energetic which captures the fun and excitement of the winning experience. The one-color posterized technique adds to the bold and graphic brand style.
- The typography combined with bright colors, posterized images and doodles differentiates the Lottery's branding from the usual glitzy and visually complicated graphic style that most lottery brands employ.
- The use of "doodles" or graphics is encouraged in campaign art that features winners, scholarship recipients, games or other relevant topics.

##### Players Club Services

- The Lottery offers a players club at MyArkansasLottery.com called The Club. By registering a free account, players gain access to Lottery news, winning number emails, the Play It Again® and Points for Prizes® programs, and special second-chance drawings. ##### Play It Again

- The Play It Again® program allows players to submit eligible, non-winning instant tickets for entry into cash prize drawings. After the last day to redeem tickets for any eligible instant game, one entry is drawn to win a monetary prize. The same mechanism gives players both their Play It Again® entries and Points for Prizes® rewards points. There were three Play It Again® drawings held during fiscal year 2017.
