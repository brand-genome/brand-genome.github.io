## Brand Guidelines

### 2020

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Billon's mission is to unleash the transformational capabilities of distributed ledger technology (DLT) with a high-performance system for trusted document management, digital acceleration, and seamless money transfers, enabling businesses and end users to benefit from secure, regulatory-compliant solutions [^1].

##### Core Values

- Regulatory compliance
- Innovation
- Human-centric technology
- Security
- Digital transformation
- Environmental responsibility

##### Target Audience

- Businesses seeking digital acceleration, transformation, and instant money transfers, as well as end users who value security and control over their data and money. The brand also targets partners in banking, energy, FMCG, and gaming sectors [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Innovative
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Empowering
- Modern

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is clean, minimalist, and modern, featuring a logo with intertwined green and blue shapes symbolizing movement, connection, and infinity. The color palette includes navy blue, green, light blue, yellow, and white, with Montserrat as the official font. Visuals emphasize clarity, balance, and professionalism, with strict guidelines for logo usage, color proportions, and iconography [^3].
