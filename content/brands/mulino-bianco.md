## Brand Guidelines

### 2022

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To evoke a rustic, natural, and artisanal feel through its products and visual identity, emphasizing tradition, nature, and wholesome values [^1].

##### Core Values

- tradition
- nature
- artisanship
- wholesomeness

##### Target Audience

- Consumers seeking authentic, natural, and artisanal bakery products, with an appreciation for rustic and wholesome experiences [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- rustic
- natural
- artisanal
- inviting
- wholesome

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses a central emblem of a traditional mill surrounded by wheat, flowers, and a bee, with a prominent blue ribbon banner. The color palette is based on natural tones (beige, blue, brown, yellow), and the visual style is balanced, clean, and inviting, emphasizing rural and artisanal themes [^3].
