## Brand Guidelines

### 2023

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Opera's mission is to provide users with a better alternative to conventional web browsers, leading the way with groundbreaking innovations that become industry standards. The brand aims to redefine the boundaries of web browsers and pioneer their capabilities, maintaining an unwavering commitment to innovation as it unveils its new look [^1].

##### Core Values

- innovation
- clarity
- modernity

- adaptability

##### Target Audience

- Users seeking a modern, innovative, and high-tech web browsing experience, likely those who value visual appeal, clarity, and advanced features [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- dynamic
- expressive
- modern
- playful
- powerful
- never boring or conservative

##### Visual Identity Overview

- Opera One's visual identity combines high-contrast black and white with a vibrant, expressive color palette and fluid, organic gradients. The design is modern, minimalistic, and high-tech, featuring sleek layouts, bold typography, and dynamic graphic elements that evoke motion and clarity. The logo and iconography are clean, recognizable, and adaptable for various contexts [^3].

##### Typography

- We use Be Vietnam Pro as the primary font for text setting throughout the branding. The typeface is a Neo Grotesk that embodies visual clarity and simplicity, which are synonymous with the attributes of Opera One.
- The family has 18 styles available but we primarily stick to regular, bold, or black.
- Space Mono, regular, is used as the secondary font and is used sparsely as both a contrast ingredient, and for utility such as headers and footnotes.
- The typesetting and layout resemble a feeling of neatly stacking and organizing elements into defined spaces. Our headlines are tightly set in capitals, negative letter spacing, and leading. The subheading and the body styles are more open for readability and legibility.
