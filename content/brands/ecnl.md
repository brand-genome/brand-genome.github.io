## Brand Guidelines

### 2020

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The ECNL's new identity was created to inspire excellence and growth in a new generation of players, and to represent the leadership, collaborative spirit and innovation that define the ECNL as the standard-bearer of excellence in youth sports. The league protects and propels the integrity of the game and everyone impacted by it by facilitating the perfect symmetry of excellence and humility, exclusivity and accessibility, freedom and community. Challenging everyone to rise to their best creates game-changers that live well, long after cleats are unlaced. ECNL will never rest on its laurels, will lead, set its eyes on new horizons, and pursue them relentlessly [^1].

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The ECNL visual identity centers on a modern, shield-shaped badge with bold, sans-serif typography and geometric iconography. The color palette includes teal, dark blue, purple, red, lime green, and other gender- and division-specific colors. The design emphasizes clean lines, symmetry, and a minimalist, sports-oriented aesthetic, with tailored programming and structure for each gender but united under one identity. Logo usage is strictly regulated for clarity and consistency across all touchpoints [^3].

##### Logo Usage

- There are three categories of logos that are approved for use by clubs participating in the ECNL: (i) ECNL Primary Logo; (ii) ECNL Regional League Badge; and (iii) Conference Badges. The usage guidelines and requirements for each category are described within this document.
- Clubs may only use the logos that reflect the level of competition in which they participate.
- Use of these logos in the colors Composure and Field Vision are limited, and must be solely for promoting the club’s general level of participation in the ECNL - either in ECNL Club Competition, ECNL Regional League Competition, or both levels.
- Clubs in the ECNL Club Competition may use the Primary Logo as indicated below.
- Clubs in the ECNL Regional League may use the Regional League Badge as indicated below.
- Primary Logo and Regional League Badge may be used in the colors Composure and Field Vision as indicated only on the following applications: • Club website home pages • Social media identifiers
- Logos in the colors Composure or Field Vision may not be used in any other manner by clubs.
- The Boys and Girls Division Logos, using the gender specific colors, are the only logos that may be used for club marketing and promotion of the specific gender programs in which the club participates.
- These logos and colors may be used in applications as varied as presentation documents, social media posts, printed or digital flyers, video, etc.
- Clubs may not use the colors Composure or Field Vision on any logos for marketing and promotion of specific gender programs and competitions.
- Conference Badges may be used by clubs in the ECNL Club Competition when referring to conference competition in which they participate.
- Conference Badges should be used in the color designated for the conference by the ECNL. They may not be used in alternative colors.
- Approved vendors will be designated by ECNL for purchase of field signage, tents, and other field accessories, with approved application of selected ECNL logos.
- Clubs must use approved vendors in order to use any ECNL logos on field signage, tents, and other field accessories.
- Maintaining clear space around the logo is vital to enable brand recognition and legibility.
- To create the necessary clear space, use the width of the "E" at the final size to ensure a consistent space.
- Minimum size guidelines should be used to ensure readability of the logo across all applications.
- Minimum size: 1"
- Minimum size: .75"
