## Brand Guidelines

### 2017

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To usher in a new era in the data center by providing fully integrated systems designed for performance, efficiency, and adaptability, aligning hardware with the needs of software and fostering a monumental leap forward in hardware technology.

##### Core Values

- performance
- logical autonomy
- inherent efficiency
- Zen balance
- adaptability
- integration
- security

##### Target Audience

- Data center architects and professionals seeking advanced solutions for modern data center challenges, including those who demand more cores, more I/O, more bandwidth, and integrated security.

##### Personality Traits

- connected
- interwoven
- alive
- adapted

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity features a modern, technological aesthetic with circular, gear-like emblems, segmented and gradient blue and gray elements, and bold, sans-serif typography. The logo is used across all media with strict guidelines for color palette, clear space, and size, emphasizing clarity, identification, and brand consistency.

#### Categories

##### Brand Voice

- Brand Personality Connected. Interwoven. Alive. Adapted.
- Brand Promise A new era in the data center.
- Brand Story The challenges of the modern data center require a different solution. The days of incremental advancements are in the past. Data center architects demand more — more cores, more I/O, more bandwidth AND seamless, integrated security. The answer is AMD EPYC. A fully integrated system created for thinking, learning and solving. AMD EPYC is a fortress of strength, symmetry and fluid power — aligning hardware with the needs of software at the dawn of this new era in the data center. The world’s work? It is powered by AMD EPYC. The AMD EPYC neural network is constantly adapting, distributing workloads intelligently and recalibrating what is possible. Built for performance with logical autonomy, inherent efficiency and Zen balance, it is next-level architecture. A monumental leap forward in hardware technology that fosters a new epoch in the data center.
