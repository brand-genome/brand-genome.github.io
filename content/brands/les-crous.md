## Brand Guidelines

### 2016

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The brand aims to provide clear, modern, and unified visual communication for the Crous network, supporting its missions in student services across France [^1].

##### Core Values

- clarity
- unity
- modernity
- accessibility

##### Target Audience

- Students, university staff, and partners of the Crous network throughout French regions [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- professional
- approachable
- innovative
- consistent

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is centered on a minimalistic, bold red circle logo with white sans-serif typography, strict color palette guidelines, regional variations, and consistent use of Montserrat and Chalet fonts. The style is clean, modern, and adaptable for print, digital, and co-branded materials [^3].
