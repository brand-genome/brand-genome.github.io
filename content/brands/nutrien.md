## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Feeding the Future: Nutrien's mission is to provide nourishment for the world's growing population and demonstrate a long-term commitment to innovation [^1].

##### Core Values

- innovation
- sustainability
- growth
- humility
- friendliness

##### Target Audience

- Audiences seeking agricultural solutions, including stakeholders in food production, crop management, and those interested in sustainable, forward-looking agricultural practices [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- progressive
- friendly
- humble
- modern
- forward-looking

##### Visual Identity Overview

- Nutrien's visual identity centers on a clean, modern logo with a stylized green leaf, a bold sans-serif font, and a color palette dominated by vibrant greens and black. The brand uses consistent graphic elements, clear space, and distinctive typography to convey freshness, growth, and professionalism [^3].

##### Typography

- The Foco font family is the preferred typeface for headlines, to be used in all high-level branding communications material. A distinctive sans serif font with a broad range of weights and styles, Foco fits the progressive personality of the Nutrien brand.
- Recommended Usage: Headlines / callouts Signage / display Stationery (design elements only)
- The Museo font family is the preferred typeface for body copy, to be used in all high-level branding communications material. A clean and easy-to-read slab serif font, it pairs well with our headline font, Foco.
- Recommended Usage: Body copy Support copy / Captions Quotations
- When Foco is not available, the Arial font family is the alternative headline typeface.
- Recommended Usage: Internal communications- Headlines / callouts Email Presentations
- Do not distort the Nutrien logo or any other Nutrien visual brand element.
- Do no redraw or substitute any part of the logo.
- Do not lock up any content with the logo in place of the approved tagline.
- Do not change the color of any part of the logo or tagline.
- Do not use colors outside of the Nutrien color system palette.
- Do not reproduce the logo smaller than minimum size.

##### Logo Usage

- The Nutrien logo without the tagline should only be used on exterior signage, clothing/uniforms, delivery vehicles and small promotional items.
- The Nutrien Logo with the tagline should be used in all communications, including advertising, brochures, letterheads, and business cards.

##### Tone And Messaging

- The Nutrien logo is the most recognizable representation of our brand. Its sans serif font is clean and modern, with the use of italics suggesting a progressive, forward-looking company. The primarily lower case lettering, with rounded-off corners on the letterforms themselves, speaks to our humble, friendly approach to business.
- The Nutrien tagline, "Feeding the Future," defines the vital role our company plays in providing nourishment for the world's growing population. It also speaks to our ongoing, long-term commitment to innovation.
