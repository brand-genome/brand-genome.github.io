## Brand Guidelines

### 2014

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Educar para pensar, decidir y servir. The brand aims to form real people, not idealized profiles, and is committed to social change, believing that a different world is possible. The invitation 'Sé real, piensa social' reflects this mission for all involved in the project [^1].

##### Core Values

- Cercana
- Humana
- Confiable
- Inteligente
- Diferente
- Investigadora

##### Target Audience

- Individuals seeking quality professional education and those interested in social transformation, with a focus on forming real, tangible people rather than abstract profiles [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Cercana
- Humana
- Confiable
- Inteligente
- Diferente
- Investigadora

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is contemporary and versatile, unified by graphic elements such as typography, shapes, sizes, and colors. The logo features a stylized 'A' with a star, using a palette of blue and yellow, and is supported by strict guidelines for usage, scale, and color. The brand employs modern sans-serif fonts and geometric forms, ensuring coherence and adaptability across applications [^3].

- La paleta está constituida por dos colores Azul (Pantone: 540C) y Amarillo (Pantone: 130C). Estos colores deberán usarse siempre que se presente el logotipo a color, ya sea impreso o en pantalla. - El logotipo podrá ser usado sobre fondo de color azul (Pantone:540C). En este caso, deberá ser implementado en blanco a excepción de la letra (A), la cual deberá ser amarilla (Pantone: 130C).

El diseñador podrá usar esta versión según la pertinencia del caso. En caso de usar otras tintas deberán ser tomadas de la carta de pantones presentada.

- No implementar colores que estén fuera de la paleta cromática establecida.
