## Brand Guidelines

### 2021

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To promote Kitamoto City as a healthy cultural city surrounded by rich greenery, encouraging migration and settlement by highlighting the city's natural beauty and relaxed lifestyle [^1].

##### Core Values

- eco-consciousness
- community
- health
- culture
- diversity
- relaxed living

##### Target Audience

- Residents, potential new settlers, and local businesses interested in a city with abundant nature, a relaxed pace, and a strong sense of community [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- friendly
- playful
- eco-friendly
- approachable
- creative

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on abstract, colorful shapes inspired by local nature, with a dominant green palette, playful compositions, and a logo featuring stylized leaf-like typography. The design emphasizes flexibility, diversity, and eco-friendliness [^3].
