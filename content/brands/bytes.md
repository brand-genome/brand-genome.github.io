##### Visual Identity Overview

- Bytes uses a pixelated, digital font reminiscent of early computer displays, with a logo composed of tiny squares to symbolize precision and building blocks. The color palette features bold neon-inspired hues (black, neon green, teal, purple) and softer secondary tones (lavender, lime, mint green, blue sky), reflecting technical expertise and creativity. Typography combines playful digital-inspired fonts, modern clean typefaces, and bold geometric styles for clarity and consistency. The overall visual style is retro, digital, and minimalistic, emphasizing geometric shapes and high contrast.
