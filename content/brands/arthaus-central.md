### 2022

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Arthaus aims to create a flexible and organic graphic identity that adapts to artistic representations, using minimal elements that transform and combine to convey sensations in each piece. The brand is guided by justified graphic and communicational decisions, emphasizing adaptability and strategic communication for future brand development [^1].

##### Core Values

- flexibility
- organic development
- justified decisions
- adaptability
- strategic communication

##### Target Audience

- Individuals and organizations interested in contemporary art, creative expression, and modern visual communication, likely including artists, designers, and cultural institutions [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- minimalist
- dynamic
- bold
- conceptual

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is minimalist, with a strong emphasis on bold, sans-serif typography, high contrast (primarily black and white), modular layouts, and flexible use of elements. The brand uses both vertical and horizontal logo variations, color palettes with principal and secondary colors, and applies its identity across various media such as flyers, social networks, business cards, and letterheads. The style is striking, clean, and adaptable, with clear rules for logo usage, color, and typography [^3].
