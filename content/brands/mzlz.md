## Brand Guidelines

### 2014

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To represent Zagreb International Airport with a strong, modern, and recognizable visual identity, emphasizing local partnerships and addressing economic challenges within the region [^1].

##### Core Values

- authenticity
- imagination
- appeal
- team value
- know-how
- diversity
- complementarity of services [^2]

##### Target Audience

- Travelers, business partners, and stakeholders engaging with Zagreb International Airport, including those seeking professional, reliable, and modern airport services [^3].

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- technical
- legible
- dynamic
- professional
- relaxed
- imaginative [^4]

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses a geometric, dynamic logo inspired by aircraft and flight, with a color palette based on Croatian and French flags, Helvetica typography for clarity and modernity, and consistent application across all materials. Visual style emphasizes clean layouts, clear logo placement, and use of primary blue for strength and stability [^5].

##### Flags

- Flag Design Concepts - The picture should feature two parallel designs of flags, each approximately 2 meters in height and 1 meter in width, displayed side by side with a small space between them. The first flag design on the left should have a solid purple vertical background with a white and blue diamond pattern in the lower portion, oriented with the diamond shapes arranged diagonally. The second flag design on the right should contain a layered, three-dimensional diamond pattern in red, gray, and navy blue shades, with the pattern appearing to have a slight perspective tilt, positioned on a white background. Below both flag designs, the text 'MZLZ' should be prominently displayed in a simple, modern font, with the first design's text in white on a purple background and the second design's text in dark gray or black on a plain white background. The entire composition should be aligned vertically on a plain, light-colored surface, with the dimensions and proportions clearly indicated through annotations, including dimensions of 2 meters in height and 1 meter in width for each flag, and segmentation lines to emphasize the 1/4 divisions in segments of the flags showing the design layout. The overall scene should be well-lit, emphasizing the colors and shapes, and should have a neutral background that highlights the flag designs.

##### Vehicle Identification

- Vehicle logo is logo which is used for Zagreb International Airport vehicles.

- In case that there is a need of setting the whole company name, while there is a lack of sufficient space for clear visibility of the logo, vehicle logo can be applied.
- Za potrebe oznacavanja auta koristi se Sekundarni logotip koji se postavlja ispod retrovizora.
- For the purposes of vehicle identification Secondary logo is used that is placed under the rearview mirror.
