## Brand Guidelines

### 2021

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Marine Life 2030 is a new program endorsed by the UN Decade of Ocean Science for Sustainable Development, aiming to reflect the complex nature of marine and coastal environments and facilitate professional and consistent representation of marine life and conservation efforts.

##### Core Values

- marine conservation
- diversity
- professionalism
- consistency

##### Target Audience

- Organizations, professionals, and stakeholders involved in marine and coastal environments, particularly those participating in the UN Decade of Ocean Science for Sustainable Development.

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- artistic
- clean
- bold

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on stylized, artistic representations of marine life, prominently featuring whales and other sea creatures in flowing, interconnected designs. The color palette includes gold, dark blue, light blue, and white, with clean, modern layouts and a thin gold border. Typography is bold and capitalized, and the logo is designed for versatility and clarity across various applications.
