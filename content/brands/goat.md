#### Brand Summary

- GOAT aims to create a sustainable cycle by offering an innovative way to share excess food within communities, with the goal of reducing food waste and leaving a better world for future generations [^1].

##### Core Values

- sustainability
- innovation
- community
- reducing food waste
- sharing

##### Target Audience

- The visual identity is defined by a custom, rounded, modern typography, vibrant color palette inspired by food, geometric shapes, and a logo that incorporates an infinity symbol to represent the sustainable cycle. ##### Typography

- Goat logo has been carefully designed using a typography developed from scratch on a grid and a set of circumferences, giving a friendly, modern and versatile appearance.
- The GOAT logotype uses a typography created to represent, in a dynamic and integral way, the company's philosophy and the interrelationship between its stakeholders.
- The typography used for GOAT is created on a grid of circumferences that have been carefully balanced.
- We have selected the Montserrat typography as the primary font to be used with the GOAT logo. It is a modern Sans Serif developed with a huge geometric simplicity. It has useful variants and an open source license.
