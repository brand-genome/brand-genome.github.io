## Brand Guidelines

### 2014

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Throughout the world, professionals work with the best assembly tools for screws and nuts from the Swiss manufacturer PB Swiss Tools. The brand aims to deliver uncompromising Swiss quality: precise, durable, and reliable tools appreciated by professionals [^1].

##### Core Values

- Outstanding
- Precision
- Reliable
- Innovation
- Sustainability and Business Excellence
- Customer Service
- Social Responsibility
- Swiss Quality

##### Target Audience

- Professionals who drive screws and nuts, including those in assembly, manufacturing, and technical fields, seeking high-quality, reliable tools [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Professional
- Precise
- Durable
- Reliable
- Innovative
- Resilient

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is characterized by a dramatic alpine mountain landscape, a clean and professional aesthetic, prominent use of red and white, minimal black, and consistent logo placement. The design emphasizes grandeur, resilience, and Swiss origin, with tools and visuals photographed on white backgrounds and a focus on clarity and memorability [^3].

#### Categories

##### Brand Imagery

- The image should feature a dramatic, alpine mountain landscape with a prominent snow-covered peak dominating the scene, set against a background of a cloudy sky with varying shades of gray and white clouds, suggesting an overcast or stormy weather. The foreground might include rugged rocks and patches of snow or ice, with a sense of scale emphasizing the mountain's height. The overall tone should evoke a sense of grandeur and coldness, aligning with the themes of corporate resilience and lofty goals. The title and branding elements, such as the red banner at the top with white text, should be visibly integrated, possibly overlaying the sky area, maintaining a clean and professional aesthetic suitable for a corporate design guidelines cover.
- The image should feature a mountainous winter landscape with snow-covered peaks and ridges extending into the distance, under a cloudy, overcast sky with thick, dark clouds dominating the scene. Foreground elements include a large, rugged, dark-colored rock partially covered with snow, positioned toward the lower left area. The background showcases multiple layers of snow-capped mountains gradually fading into the horizon, suggesting depth and vastness. The upper right section displays prominently a large, bold, red numeral '1,' contrasting sharply with the cloudy sky. Adjacent to the numeral, slightly below and to the left, is the text 'BRAND CODE' in white, capitalized letters, positioned horizontally. The overall tone of the image is cold and dramatic, with a focus on the contrast between the dark skies, the white snow, and the vivid red number to catch attention.
- The image should contain a central circle labeled 'VISION' with a bold, dark text and a thick red border around it, containing the text: 'Throughout the world, professionals work with the best assembly tools for screws and nuts from the Swiss manufacturer PB Swiss Tools.' There should be four additional circles arranged around the central one, each with a thin gray border and labeled with different attributes: 'OUTSTANDING' at the top, with text about producing the best tool; 'PRECISION' on the left side, describing the importance of a precision tool for effective and injury-free work; 'RELIABLE' at the bottom, emphasizing trustworthiness of employees and products with a lifetime guarantee; and one more circle at the top that overlaps slightly with the 'OUTSTANDING' circle, labeled also 'OUTSTANDING', with text about producing very good tools, but only one produces the best. All circles should be evenly spaced and proportioned, with the central one slightly more prominent. The background should be plain or lightly shaded to make the circles stand out, and there are no additional graphical elements or images. Text inside each circle is centered and neatly formatted.
- The image features a hierarchical diagram representing the organizational structure of a Swiss Tools company. At the top, there is a red header labeled 'SWISS TOOLS'. Below this is the company's motto, 'Work with the best.', aligned centrally. The structure branches into two main categories: 'QUALITY HAND TOOLS (QHT)' on the left and 'MEDICAL INSTRUMENTS (MI)' on the right, with a shared node labeled 'BRAND LEVEL' connecting them. Under 'QUALITY HAND TOOLS (QHT)', several subcategories are present: 'Color Code', which further branches into 'Rainbow', 'Personal Line', and 'Tools color coded'; 'Screwdrivers' with subcategories 'Classic', 'Multitool', and 'SwissGrip'; 'Key L-wrenches' with 'Metric mm', 'Holding ring', and 'Inch'; 'Striking tools' including 'Mallets', 'Centre punches and pin punches', and 'Chisels'; 'Special tools' with 'Special Tools Universal and file handles'; 'Interchangeable handles and blades. PrecisionBits' that includes 'PrecisionBits', 'PrecisionBits accessories', and 'Socket wrench bits'; and 'Sales promotion' containing 'POS', 'Tented table', and 'Sales stands'. Accompanying text labels and subcategories are in a consistent rectangular box format, aligned horizontally and vertically with clear indentation to indicate hierarchy. The diagram's background is plain, with lines connecting the boxes to illustrate relationships, and the text within the boxes is black against a white background, ensuring clarity and readability.
