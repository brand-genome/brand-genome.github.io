## Brand Guidelines

### 2016

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Crafted with passion from the heart of paradise. We are an innovative and independent brewery. Welcome to the ‘ohana. Consistent portrayal of the Maui Brewing Co. brand across all mediums is critical to building and maintaining brand recognition and establishing a strong coherent message across all audience touchpoints [^1].

##### Core Values

- passion
- care
- innovation
- independence
- uncompromising quality
- honoring the island
- community ('ohana')

##### Target Audience

- Consumers and staff who value bold, engaging narratives, Hawaiian craft, and independent brewing. The brand aims to connect with those who appreciate quality, authenticity, and the spirit of Hawaii [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- fiercely independent
- innovative
- passionate
- authentic
- community-oriented
- bold

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is crafted with care and passion, featuring tribal and Polynesian motifs, bold and modern typography, a limited color palette dominated by black, white, and accent colors, and flexible logo lockups. Packaging and environmental design incorporate Hawaiian cultural elements, geometric patterns, and illustrations by a Polynesian tattoo artist, ensuring a cohesive and authentic brand presence across all touchpoints [^3].
