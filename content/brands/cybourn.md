### 2021

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- CyBourn is a global cybersecurity company with the mission to address challenges in technologies and operations in cyberspace. We increase protection by providing forward-thinking transparent services for threat detection, prevention, and response [^1].

##### Core Values

- effective service delivery
- leveraging technology
- enabling innovation
- high standards in information security
- quality
- IT service management certifications
- strategic security partnership
- cutting-edge cyber protection

##### Target Audience

- Global, regional, and national enterprises across private sector, public sector, and governmental bodies, seeking strategic cybersecurity solutions [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- confident
- positive
- sophisticated
- forward-thinking
- transparent
- adaptable

##### Visual Identity Overview

- CyBourn's visual identity is instantly recognizable, built on a strong, modern design scheme with a customized wordmark and a distinctive eye symbol as a secondary mark. The brand uses a palette of blues and accent colors, clean typography (Manrope and Bebas Neue), and imagery that emphasizes modernity, diversity, and digital sophistication. Consistency, clarity, and contrast are prioritized in all visual elements [^3].
