## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To provide a flexible framework of practical guidelines that ensures global consistency for the Year of South Australian Wine brand identity, whilst providing scope for creativity and initiative [^1].

##### Core Values

- global consistency
- creativity
- initiative

##### Target Audience

- Individuals and organizations involved in promoting South Australian wine, including event organizers, marketers, and designers seeking to implement the brand identity across various applications [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- playful
- joyful
- authoritative
- celebratory
- modern

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity features bold, stylized numerals integrated with wine-related motifs, a playful use of color (pink, French Navy, red), and abstract food and drink patterns. The logo and brandmark are designed for clarity, impact, and flexibility, with illustrative graphics and merchandise applications reinforcing a vibrant, modern, and celebratory theme [^3].

##### Typography

- Komu should be used for headings and feature text. Komu is an Adobe font and can be downloaded from Adobe fonts.
- Proxima Nova should be used for longer bodies of text and sub-headings. Proxima Nova’s different weights can be used throughout applications to create hierarchy, however, it is recommended to primarily use Light and Bold. Proxima Nova is available through Adobe Typekit or can be purchased via MyFonts.

##### Logo Usage

- The 2021 the Year of South Australian Wine logo is the principal symbo. It comprises two elements: the 2021 brandmark and the YOW logotype.
- These have been designed to work together and their scale and proportions have been carefully designed to allow maximum clarity, impact and flexibility of use.
- The primary logo is the preferred logo and should be used in all instances where possible.
- Minimum size standards based on height have been developed to ensure that the YOW logos are reproduced correctly at a small size.
- The brandmark should be used when the logo is required to be reproduced smaller than the minimum size.
- MINIMUM SIZE Primary logo or SOUTH minimum size: /AUSTRALIAN 15mm/50px high 'l• '111HEAR I WINE 2% 'saur AusTRAAN WINE (6 Secondary logo minimum size: 14mm/45px high 2A
- The isolation area is the space around the logo in which no other elements must encroach. The dimensions of the isolation area are formed by using the height of the 'WINE' from the YOW logo.
- The YOW logo must never be re-drawn or adjusted and should only be reproduced from logo files provided.
- The logo should not appear in any other colour than the supplied formats.
- The logotype should not be reproduced in any other typeface.
- Avoid using low resolution versions of the logo. If other file types are required please contact the YOW creative team.
- The logo should not be stretched, condensed or re-proportioned in any way.
