## Brand Guidelines

### 2015

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To provide accessible, coherent, and harmonized public services for all citizens, emphasizing social connection and solidarity.

##### Core Values

- lien
- accompagnement
- accessibilité
- services pour tous les citoyens

##### Target Audience

- All citizens seeking public services, with a focus on inclusivity and accessibility.

##### Personality Traits

- professional
- approachable
- inclusive
- supportive

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a stylized logo featuring two abstract human figures forming an 'M', symbolizing connection and solidarity. The color palette uses dark blue and red, with clean, minimalistic layouts and bold, sans-serif typography. The logo is used in both vertical and horizontal variants, with strict rules for size, color, and protection zones to ensure consistency across all communication materials.
