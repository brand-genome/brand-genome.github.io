#### Brand Summary

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The Conservative Party of Canada brand is defined by a clean, modern, and professional visual identity. The primary logo features a stylized letter 'C' in dark or bright blue, with a red maple leaf at its center, symbolizing Canadian identity. The logo is used in both stacked and horizontal versions, with bold, uppercase sans-serif typography for the party name. The color palette includes navy blue (Pantone 295), bright blue (Pantone 2995), red (Pantone 185), and additional tertiary colors such as purple, brown, and green. The overall style emphasizes clarity, strength, and patriotism, with consistent use of the maple leaf and blue tones across all visual elements [^1].
