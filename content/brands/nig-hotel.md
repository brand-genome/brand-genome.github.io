### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To establish a unified and recognizable corporate image for Navigator International Group Hotel, symbolizing diversified business growth and the pursuit of dreams, represented by the sailboat motif and the rising sun [^1].

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a modern, sans-serif logo with a stylized sailboat, using a color palette of Navigate Brown and Navigate Black, complemented by auxiliary colors. The design emphasizes clarity, boldness, minimalism, and harmonious unity, with strict guidelines for logo usage, typography, layout, and composition to ensure consistency across all applications [^3].
