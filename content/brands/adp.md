##### Core Values

- dependability
- advanced methodology
- progress

##### Target Audience

- Individuals and organizations seeking innovative, people-centered solutions for the workplace.

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- forward-oriented
- dependable

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a bold, stylized logo with solid letters and a modern face, primarily using ADP Red and ADP Navy colors. The logo is designed for instant recognition, with clear guidelines for usage, minimum size, clear space, and background color. The tagline 'Always Designing for People' reinforces the brand's people-centric approach.
