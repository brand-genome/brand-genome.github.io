##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on stylized jet aircraft imagery, bold wordmarks, shield emblems, and a consistent color palette of navy blue, maroon/red, and light blue. Typography is unified and modern, with fonts like Legacy (Modified), Champion, and Lato. The style emphasizes speed, movement, and athleticism, with clean, dynamic layouts and minimal backgrounds [^3].

##### Brand Imagery

- The image should feature a stylized, dynamic illustration of an airplane depicted in a modern, graphic style emphasizing movement and speed, set against a dark blue background. The airplane is outlined with bold white lines, with interior fill colors predominantly in shades of navy blue, accented by a maroon section on the tail and light blue highlights on the wings and fuselage, creating a sense of depth and dimension. The illustration captures a side view of the airplane, showing the streamlined shape of the fuselage, the wings extending outward with slight upward angles, the tail fin slightly angled backward, and a cockpit area outlined in white. The design should convey an athletic, energetic feel suitable for a guide related to sports or performance, consistent with an 'Athletics Style Guide' theme, with minimal additional details or background elements, focusing entirely on the stylized aircraft.

##### Brand Values

- The number three is a significant number to the history of Newman University. Founded in 1933 by the Adorers of the Blood of Christ, the ASC Religious Sisters logo features three blood drops inside the heart of their logo. Three represents Newman University's strong Catholic faith and values in our belief in the Holy Trinity, the unity of Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, as three persons in one God. Throughout our growth the university has had three names: Sacred Heart, Kansas Newman College, and Newman University.
- The three serifs in the Newman Word mark are a symbolic gesture to the number three and Newman University's history.
