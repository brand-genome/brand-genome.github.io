## Brand Guidelines

### 2016

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To achieve a powerfully consistent effect in Sewanee Athletics branding by applying visual elements according to established guidelines, supporting the identity of Sewanee Tigers and fostering school spirit and recognition.

##### Core Values

- consistency
- recognition
- school spirit
- proper application of visual elements

##### Target Audience

- Athletic department members, partners, and anyone designing materials for Sewanee Athletics, including students, staff, and fans.

##### Personality Traits

- bold
- collegiate
- athletic
- strong
- recognizable

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on the Tiger S as the primary mark and the Sewanee Tiger as the secondary mark, with a color palette of purple, Vegas Gold, white, and black. Superstar M54 is the exclusive font, and logo usage is strictly defined to ensure consistency. The style is bold, collegiate, and athletic, with clear guidelines for color, typography, and logo presentation.
