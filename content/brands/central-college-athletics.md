## Brand Guidelines

### 2017

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To raise Central College's profile in a competitive marketplace by employing a graphic identity that is bright, visually appealing, unique, and distinctive, reflecting the powerful championships legacy of Central College athletics and maintaining brand discipline for a consistent, recognizable look that enhances the institutional brand and helps Central athletics stand out.

##### Core Values

- consistency
- recognizability
- discipline
- distinctiveness
- legacy

##### Target Audience

- Apparel, gear, signage, and materials associated with the Central College athletics department, including student-athletes, athletics staff, and vendors producing branded merchandise.

##### Personality Traits

- competitive
- authoritative
- robust
- bold
- modern

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on strong geometric shapes, clean lines, and a bold color palette of red (Pantone 185), white, and cool gray (Pantone 3). Logos feature prominent block-style 'C' letters and stylized lion emblems, with strict guidelines for color usage, typography (Encode Sans Wide), and logo placement. The style is minimalistic, modern, and designed for clarity and impact across uniforms, merchandise, and promotional materials.

#### Categories

##### Color Palette

- The athletics identity colors are Red Pantone 185 and white. Cool Gray Pantone 3 is used as an accent color. The identity is not to be used in any other colors.
- Only red or white uniform colors are permitted. Exceptions are made for baseball/softball uniforms where traditional road gray uniforms are allowed.
- In unique situations, the athletics director may grant exceptions for gray as an alternate uniform color.
- In addition to red and white, gray may be used as an alternate color. Each are considered brand-aligned. Multiple shades of gray are permitted, including dark/smoky gray. There are dark and light background versions of each athletics mark. When using gray, please determine if the gray is dark or light and then choose the appropriate version of the athletics mark.
- Black is not a school color and it is preferred that it not be used, but there are situations where options are limited and it is permitted, with approval.
- If gear/apparel is produced in any other color, the athletics logo and specified font may not be used.
