## Brand Guidelines

### 2017

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Bombora is the leading provider of B2B demographic, firmographic, and intent data, aiming to help marketers find in-market customers and harness the power of intent data for business growth and ROI.

##### Core Values

- trustworthy
- friendly
- clarity
- simplicity
- professionalism

##### Target Audience

- B2B marketers and sales teams seeking data-driven solutions for identifying in-market customers and improving campaign performance.

##### Personality Traits

- trustworthy
- friendly
- approachable
- dynamic
- professional

##### Visual Identity Overview

- Bombora's visual identity centers on coastal and ocean imagery, a modern rounded sans-serif wordmark, a blue and orange color palette, clear logo usage rules, and the use of textures and brushstrokes to add emotion and personality. Typography is consistent and legible, and photography emphasizes beach, ocean, and wave scenes with overlays for clarity and brand voice.

#### Categories

##### Brand Imagery

- Bombora uses beach, ocean, and wave imagery to bring our communications to life. A semi-transparent color overlay (either solid or gradient) is applied to ensure consistent brand voice and legibility of headings.
- We have identified three different categories of images: strong waves, beaches, and calm waves.

##### Typography

- Our typeface is Trebuchet MS, designed in 1996 by Vincent Connare for Microsoft Corporation. It has a trustworthy yet friendly appearance and is highly legible on screen and in print.
- Trebuchet Regular
- Trebuchet Italic
- Trebuchet Bold
- Our typeface is Open Sans, designed in 2011 by Steve Matteson for Google Fonts. It has a trustworthy yet friendly appearance and is highly legible on screen and in print.
- Open Sans Regular
- Open Sans Semibold Italic
- Open Sans Extrabold
- When overlaying text on an image, please set the text color to white (#FFFFFFF) and use a bold font weight for headings.
