## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To provide a fun and personal experience through customizable avatars and digital expression tools.

##### Core Values

- fun
- personalization
- creativity
- community
- safety

##### Target Audience

- Individuals seeking playful, personalized digital avatars and expressive tools, including users of Snapchat and related platforms.

##### Personality Traits

- friendly
- playful
- approachable
- creative

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The Bitmoji brand features a vibrant, playful visual style with a focus on minimalistic, cartoon-like avatars, a distinctive green brand color, multicolored wordmark bar, and clean, modern typography. Visual assets emphasize clarity, vibrancy, and a digital, approachable aesthetic.
