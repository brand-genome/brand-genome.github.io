##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses a modern, stylish visual identity with elegant cursive script fonts, a warm and inviting color palette (deep blue, gold, mustard, coral, off white), and imagery reflecting community, diversity, and casual urban style. Logos are clean, professional, and adaptable for both recreational and medical markets. Merchandise and illustrations feature rounded brush styles and halftone shading, reinforcing a casual, retro, and approachable vibe.
