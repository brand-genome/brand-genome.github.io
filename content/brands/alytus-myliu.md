## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Alytus is a family city, where entrepreneurial and economic centers are distributed to meet community needs, foster development, and utilize existing urban infrastructure. The vision is to become the business center of Southern Lithuania, but this is only possible through community love for the city. Alytus is a socially responsible city, caring for the well-being of its community and reborn as a better place to live [^1].

##### Core Values

- family
- community
- understanding
- safety
- comfort
- social responsibility
- love for the city

##### Target Audience

- Residents and families of Alytus, the broader community, and those seeking a socially responsible, family-oriented urban environment [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- warm
- united
- aesthetic
- safe
- inviting
- dynamic
- modern

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is based on a modern, vibrant, and dynamic aesthetic, with a stylized logo featuring smooth, flowing lines and a green color palette. The logo and graphic elements are inspired by the Nemunas river bends and emphasize unity, esthetics, and a sense of discovery. The brand uses four main colors, specific fonts, and geometric patterns to reinforce its identity across various applications [^3].
