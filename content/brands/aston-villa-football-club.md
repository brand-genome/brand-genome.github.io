## Brand Guidelines

### 2015

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- With a pioneering spirit, built on our inspirational past, we have a commitment to growth and success, to build a brighter future. Now is our time, to reignite the passion and pride that was and is Aston Villa. Everything we do, we do with passion, pride, and purpose [^1].

##### Core Values

- Better Together
- Be Brave
- Go Beyond
- Show Leadership
- Passion
- Pride
- Purpose

##### Target Audience

- Fans, partners, and those around the club, with a focus on collective celebration, unity, and engagement [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Determined
- Positive
- Passionate
- Proud
- Prepared
- Responsible
- Accountable
- Self-reliant
- Leadership spirit
- Can-do attitude

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on the lion symbol, club colors (claret, blue, yellow, black, white), bold and contrasting palettes, and clear logo usage guidelines. Imagery includes hero shots of players, roaring stances, and energetic crowd scenes, with a bespoke typeface and heraldic illustration style [^3].

#### Categories

##### Brand Voice

- Passion, pride and purpose. These three qualities underpin everything we do. You can feel them the moment you step into Villa Park, the moment our Villans step onto the pitch. Wherever we are, and whatever we do, our passion, pride and purpose always shine through.
- The way we write needs to live up to this. Like our actions, our words need to excite and inspire.
- Remember: passion, pride and purpose aren’t just buzzwords. They tell a story about how we do things, and who we are. That’s why it’s so important we live up to them.
- Football is all about passion – and football at Villa Park is no exception. So let’s make our passion part of our voice.
- We’re proud of who we are. We’re proud of our history, our fans, and the league we helped found.
- This pride is the starting point for our voice.
- Purpose is about making everything we do count. This includes what we say.
- Before “We play Man U today. Let’s hope it’s a win.” After “It’s matchday. We’re hungry.”
- Before “Join in on the action. Watch every game here on avtv.” After “Roar with us. Witness the action here on avtv.”

##### Brand Imagery

- This 'fighting like lions' spirit has shaped the photography we use. Hero shots of players usually involve roaring stances and an explosion of ferocity The treatment further intensifies these.
- Our lion will appear in a large variety of different states, in the form of background visuals. Starting with a bas relief visual to be used within the core brand.
- The lion's claws have been re-introduced. This ferocity transfers onto other design materials. Icons and our bespoke typeface for example. This could further influence an AVFC illustration style.

##### Logo Usage

- The AVFC crest should only ever be reproduced as it is supplied. and placed on clean non-busy background. It can be placed on any background as long as the integrity and minimum clear space are maintained at all times.
- The AVFC crest should not be distorted. cropped. rotated or used as a watermark. Please do not remove elements or apply special effects like drop shadows. bevels. sketch, 3D or watercolour.
- Do not place the crest on busy or colourful backgrounds that hinder clarity and legibility of the logo.
- A minimum clear area of at least the width of the 'AV' from the Crest should appear around the logo lock-up whenever it is used.
- AVFC Minimum Height Print:35mm Web: 130px
- The size and shape of the Partner logo will determine whether the 'Partner copy' will sit over one or two lines. The logo and copy will align centrally to the AVFC crest without exceeding its height.
- Mono Option (use rarely) There may be a requirement for a mono composite logo. This is based on Option One, but would incorporate the box from Option Two if needed. Only to be used on rare occasions when printing in black and white. Available on request.
- Stacked Option (Use Rarely) In rare cases, for odd shaped designs, a stacked option may be needed. Available on request.
- Use of the composite logo is always subject to approval from the Club.
