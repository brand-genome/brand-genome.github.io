##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on the Kaaba, using black and gold as primary colors, with modern, elegant, and elite attributes. The logo is a stylized Arabic word 'Al-Rehal' intertwined to resemble the Kaaba, employing geometric shapes, negative space, and solid block colors. Serif and sans-serif font families are used for Arabic and English, and imagery focuses on feelings, luxury, and the Kaaba. The identity system includes logo variations, responsive logos, icon sets, and applications across digital, print, uniforms, and interior/exterior branding [^3].

##### Logo Usage

- The logo concept revolves around the Arabic word "Al-Rehal" creatively designed in a way that the letters are intricately intertwined, resembling the structure of the Holy Kaaba when viewed from a specific angle, complete with its distinctive details. The term "Al-Rehal" in Arabic signifies both preparation for travel and reaching a destination. This idea is symbolically depicted in the logo, where the overall shape forms an upward arrow, representing the direction and aspiration toward the Holy Kaaba, which serves as the ultimate destination.
- The logo construction was a meticulous process that involved precise calculations of its geometric dimensions to achieve a captivating final form. The aspect ratio of the symbol's overall shape was determined using the golden ratio, contributing to its aesthetic appeal and visual harmony.
- The Golden Ratio is 1 : 1.618
- There are various logo variations designed to suit different scenarios. The primary logo is intended for universal use, while the logotype is specifically tailored for narrow, strip-like spaces. Additionally, the standalone icon can be used independently.
- The logo incorporates the colors of the Holy Kaaba, primarily black and golden (as a secondary hue), chosen for their profound significance in representing the Holy Kaaba.
