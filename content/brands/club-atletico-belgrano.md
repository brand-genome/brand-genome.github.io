## Brand Guidelines

### 2016

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Consolidate the image of Belgrano through unified visual identity and communication, serving as a living tool for corporate and product applications, and guiding creative possibilities to communicate the club's essence [^1].

##### Core Values

- unity
- creativity
- identity
- attention to detail

##### Target Audience

- Members, supporters, and the broader community associated with Club Atlético Belgrano [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- authentic
- proud
- modern
- creative

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is defined by a clean, symmetrical, and minimalistic logo, consistent use of color (blue, black, white), strict logo usage rules, and specified typography, ensuring high visibility, legibility, and contrast in all applications [^3].
