##### Visual Identity Overview

- The Dallas Cowboys brand visual identity centers on a bold, geometric five-pointed star as the primary mark, rendered in navy blue with white borders. The logotype features large, uppercase, block-style letters in dark blue or light gray, emphasizing strength and clarity. Uniforms and helmets are strictly color-guided using specific PANTONE and process color codes for navy, silver, royal, and silver-green, ensuring consistent reproduction across all branded materials. The overall style is minimalistic, high-contrast, and focused on clean, symmetrical compositions, with no extraneous elements or decorations [^1].
