## Brand Guidelines

### 2002

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To create consistency in the application of the new visual identity programme for Harvard Design School, ensuring all visual communications reflect a unified and professional image.

##### Core Values

- consistency
- clarity
- professionalism
- modernity

##### Target Audience

- Faculty, staff, students, and external partners of Harvard Design School, as well as recipients of official communications and branded materials.

##### Personality Traits

- minimalist
- structured
- academic
- innovative

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is defined by a minimalist, geometric logo, a custom wordmark, a restrained color palette (including PMS Warm Red, PMS 7533, Warm Gray 11, Black 6, Cool Gray 7), and precise typographic standards. Layouts emphasize clarity, hierarchy, and ample white space, with strict rules for logo and wordmark usage to maintain brand integrity across all materials.
