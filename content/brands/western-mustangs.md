## Brand Guidelines

### 2016

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To engage the mind, elevate the spirit, and promote excellence in all aspects of athletic and academic achievement, while valuing tradition and innovating for a strong future. The brand aims to bring people together and connect them socially and emotionally as a 'Mustang'.

##### Core Values

- tradition
- innovation
- excellence
- leadership
- personal growth

##### Target Audience

- Students, student-athletes, staff, recreation facility users, Sport Western campers, and the broader university community, including potential students.

##### Personality Traits

- energetic
- bold
- athletic
- dynamic
- honest
- real

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on the Mustangs logo, featuring a dynamic white mustang horse within a bold purple 'W', with a curved banner reading 'MUSTANGS'. The primary colors are Western purple and white, with gray as a supplement. Typography includes Benton Sans, Aachen Bold, and BigNoodleTitling. Professional photography of real Western people is emphasized, and all materials must maintain high quality and consistency across print, web, uniforms, merchandise, and signage.

Where possible, place the logos in opposite top corners of the exhibition panels and banners – and always follow the graphic standards guidelines included in the Basic Elements section of this document. Please consult with Mustangs Sports Properties for presentation or exhibition materials.
