## Brand Guidelines

### 2017

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To represent Icelandic’s heritage and quality in the fishing industry, emphasizing immaculate quality and sustainable fish and seafood, rooted in the historical fishing nation of Iceland and 75 years of experience.

##### Core Values

- quality
- heritage
- sustainability
- trust
- contemporary relevance

##### Target Audience

- Seafood suppliers and customers globally who value quality, sustainability, and Icelandic heritage.

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- efficient
- forward-thinking
- trustworthy
- minimalistic

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on bold, minimalistic design elements, including a corporate logo, communication logo, and brand symbol inspired by Icelandic landscapes. The color palette features dark blue, gentle gold, muted silver, and teal, with geometric sans-serif typography (Futura) and structured logo usage. Packaging design emphasizes clean layouts, cool tones, and geometric motifs.

#### Categories

- We have specified a region surrounding both elements of the communication logo that cannot be filled with design elements, text, etc. This ensures that our logo and brand symbol are prominent and consistent on all communication material.
- The Icelandic communication logo has been introduced to take further advantage of our Icelandic heritage and open the brand up to a multi-category adventure.
