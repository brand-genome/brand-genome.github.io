##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is built around bold, dynamic layouts featuring the Morgan Bear mascot and the university's iconic architectural symbols. The color palette is strictly defined with Morgan Blue, Morgan Orange, Morgan Brown, Morgan Gold, black, and white. Logos are used in specific color combinations and layouts, with clear guidelines for safe zones, minimum sizes, and background contrast. Typography is consistent, using specified fonts and styles for university and athletic marks, and all visual elements emphasize clarity, strength, and institutional pride.
