## Brand Guidelines

### 2021

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To position payment as the beginning of a deeper, richer customer experience and connection, viewing payment as the start of a long journey with the customer [^1].

##### Core Values

- connection
- evolution
- quality
- recognition
- consistency

##### Target Audience

- Clients seeking enhanced payment experiences and connected service lines, likely within the Natixis Payments family and broader business sectors [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- warm
- affectionate
- professional
- recognizable
- human

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a stylized 'D' logo, a warm and vibrant yellow as the lead color, supported by white and silver backgrounds, with strict logo usage rules, clearspace, minimum sizes, and consistent typography. Imagery is warm, celebratory, and human, with guidelines for photography, graphic devices, and applications across various media [^3].

##### Logo Usage

- Our logo is made up of two key elements, the symbol and logotype. These two elements can be separated in application, creating a strong and recognisable stamp and mark of quality.
- Logo clearspace: Minimum space around the logo
- x = Height of logotype 'D'
- Colour: Acceptable logo colour variants, text navy or white
- Photograph: Always ensure there is sufficient contrast for the logo to stand out
- Minimum recommended sizes: Based on the height of the full logo Print — 6.5mm Web — 40px
- Always carry out print and screen tests when reproducing the logo at sizes smaller than the minimum recommended.
