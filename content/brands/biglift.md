##### Logo Usage

- BigLift’s logo is never to be used otherwise than as the examples in this manual. The blue / black BigLift logo is the main form, preferably to be used on a white background. On promotional matters a yellow background (PMS109C) can be allowed sometimes. In case the background is dark (photographs, clothing etc.) the blue / white or completely white version of the logo must be used.
- The Biglift logo is to be surrounded by a clear zone -the 'Bounding box'. The 'Bounding box' allows the Biglift logo to stand apart from other images or text and therefore to appear clearly visible and authoritative at all times. The 'Bounding box' is defined by the height of the lettering within the panel, the 'X' height.
- "BigLift is KEY IN HEAVY LIFT". This tagline should be used with the logo on exhibition booths, advertisements, brochures and other promotional material. The tagline is not to be used on stationery. There are three ways to use the tagline in combination with the logo. Below is the preferred version. As an alternative, there are two more options in case the dimensions of the object the logo is used on does not allow the use of the preferred version.
- If the Biglift logo is used on long and narrow items such as pens or key cords, the tagline may be placed after the logo.
- If the Biglift logo is used in a small size, "KEY IN HEAVY LIFT" will be illegible. On clothing or sails, for example, the Biglift logo with the large tagline is used.
