## Brand Guidelines

### 2019

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Facilitate the correct use and application of the Kraft brand in all possible scenarios, supporting creativity while maintaining brand integrity and visibility [^1].

##### Core Values

- creativity
- clarity
- consistency
- visibility
- integrity

##### Target Audience

- Individuals responsible for interpreting, articulating, communicating, and applying the Kraft brand in various expressions and contexts [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- creative
- modern
- bold
- dynamic
- professional

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is characterized by bold geometric forms, a vibrant magenta color palette, a distinctive asterisk symbol, and modern sans-serif typography (Montserrat and Roboto). The logo is always used indivisibly, with strict guidelines for color, size, and application across various media. The style emphasizes clarity, contrast, and creative expression [^3].

#### Categories

##### Logo Usage

- El logosímbolo debe utilizarse siempre de forma indivisible a la hora de firmar o aplicarse en cualquier soporte gráfico.
- Ha de determinarse un área de seguridad que asegurará una perfecta visibilidad con respecto a los textos y símbolos. Esta área asegura la integridad del logosímbolo en todas las expresiones y formatos posibles.
- El tamaño mínimo al que será reproducido el logotipo será de 20mm.
