## Brand Guidelines

### 2016

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To advance the role of the Chief Marketing Officer through high-level knowledge exchange, thought leadership, and personal relationship building among senior marketing and brand decision-makers. The CMO Council is an exclusive, influential peer-networking group dedicated to furthering the stature, credibility, influence, and understanding of the strategic marketing function among business executives, opinion leaders, and critical stakeholders [^1].

##### Core Values

- exclusivity
- influence
- thought leadership
- credibility
- strategic marketing
- peer networking
- global reach
- professional advancement

##### Target Audience

- Senior marketing and brand decision-makers, authority leaders in marketing, CEOs, board members, legislators, media, analysts, and other functional specialists. Members are drawn from the upper echelons of corporate management, forming a trusted, close-knit community of peers [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- bold
- clever
- practical
- modern
- sophisticated

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is clean, modern, and impactful, emphasizing clarity and professionalism. It features a bold red color palette, custom logotype, and geometric logo elements. Imagery is bold, clever, practical, and modern, with a preference for flat, minimalistic graphic illustrations and sharp, crisp photography. Typography is consistent across print, web, and office documents, and iconography is uniform in stroke weight and proportion [^3].

##### Typography

- The Gotham font family is the primary font to be used for brand collateral and other print materials. Gotham Thin should be used for secondary headlines and body. Gotham Bold is to be used for all main headlines
- Used for Heading 1 and Heading 2 Most often in CMOC Red, White, or as a watermark
- Gotham Medium Used for Heading 3 and Heading 4 Titles Most often in CMOC Red, White, or Black.
- Gotham Thin Used for body copy, image and chart captions in CMOC Dark Grey
- Open Sans is the primary font family to be used on the CMO Council website The weights and sizes vary to define its uses When designing anything specific to the CMO Council brand for the website, be sure to follow these guidelines pertaining to typography.
- Open Sans Semibold Used for headlines and buttons
- Open Sans Regular Used for subheads and highlighted text
- Open Sans Light Used for body copy and captions
- For the office documents such as Microsoft Word and Powerpoints, we require usage of Calibri font to guarantee the consistency accross all systems It also provides editing accessibility for non-creative teams and individuals
- Calibri Bold Used only for documents that will be handed off to non-creative teams to edit (ie, PowerPoint presentations)
- Calibri Regular Used only for documents that will be handed off to non-creative teams to edit (ie, PowerPoint presentations)
- In the rare instance that a font becomes part of a graphical illustration in a multimedia or campaign communication it may become necessary to use a font other than CMO Council main font In these cases, always ensure you are using a high quality font that complements CMO Council brand fonts
