## Brand Guidelines

### 2019

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To promote art and culture, with a focus on American art and cultural exchange, through a professional and modern institution dedicated to openness and engagement.

##### Core Values

- exchange
- openness
- identity
- belonging
- clarity
- professionalism

##### Target Audience

- Individuals interested in art and culture, including artists, performers, audiences, and the broader community seeking cultural engagement and American cultural experiences.

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- professional
- open
- dynamic
- creative

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is clean, minimalistic, and modern, featuring geometric patterns, bold colors (red, blue, white), and a logo that synthesizes American symbolism and architectural elements. The brand uses consistent typography, structured layouts, and adaptable graphic codes for various applications.

#### Categories

##### Brand Imagery

- Plain White Background - The image should be a simple, plain white background with no objects, text, or other elements present. It should be clean, minimalistic, and free of any markings, shadows, or textures, serving as a neutral backdrop.
- Geometric Pattern with Red Lines - The image should feature a repeating geometric pattern with alternating rows of stylized shapes resembling the letter 'E' or 'L' in a navy blue color, positioned at varying angles to create a dynamic, tessellated effect. Intersecting these shapes are horizontal red lines and diagonal red stripes arranged in a consistent grid, forming a rhythmic, striped design. The background is white, providing a high contrast that accentuates the boldness of the pattern. The pattern is uniformly distributed across the entire space, aligned to form a seamless, regular tessellation with no visible gaps or irregularities, creating a visually striking, rhythmic pattern suitable for fabric, wallpaper, or graphic design.
- American Center Logo Design - The image should feature the phrase 'AMERICAN CENTER' prominently displayed in large, bold, black uppercase letters as the central element. Above this text, there should be a stylized American flag icon consisting of a small rectangular representation with horizontal red and white stripes and a blue canton with white stars, positioned on the top left side. The background should be white, providing high contrast for the text and flag icon. Below the main title, the phrase 'FOR ART & CULTURE' should be included in smaller, uppercase black letters, centered beneath the main text. The overall composition should be clean and minimalistic, emphasizing the institution's name and purpose, with a professional and modern aesthetic that clearly conveys the focus on American art and culture.
- Empty White Canvas - The image is entirely blank and white, with no discernible objects, subjects, text, or visual elements present. It appears as a plain, featureless, white canvas or background, with uniform color and no textures, patterns, or shading. There are no signs of scenery, figures, symbols, or partitions, and the borders are not explicitly defined, contributing to its minimalist and empty appearance.
