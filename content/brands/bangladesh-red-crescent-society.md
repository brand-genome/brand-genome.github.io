## Brand Guidelines

### 2022

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To promote and reduce human suffering, mobilizing the full strength and resources of humanity in Bangladesh and globally, as a dynamic and modern humanitarian organization [^1].

##### Core Values

- Humanitarianism
- Coherence
- Modernity
- Recognition
- Community Service
- Global Activism [^2]

##### Target Audience

- BDRCS staff and volunteers, Red Cross Red Crescent Partners, External Partners, Funding Partners, Media, Public .

##### Personality Traits

- Dynamic
- Modern
- Coherent
- Recognizable
- Community-focused

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is built around the Red Crescent symbol, using solid colors of green and red to represent Bangladesh and global activism. The brand employs a bright, clean, modern, and sophisticated look, with clear logo usage rules, a defined color palette, and consistent typography. Visual elements are applied across stationery, event materials, merchandise, equipment, vehicles, and digital mediums [^2].
