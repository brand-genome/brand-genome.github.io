## Brand Guidelines

### 2021

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- We work towards enhancing the resilience and wellbeing of communities by providing a principled and rights-based humanitarian response to overcome natural or man-made threats, risks and hazards [^1].

##### Core Values

- Compassion
- Commitment
- Impartiality

##### Target Audience

- Crisis-affected people in northern Syria and Iraq, including diverse communities with different backgrounds and beliefs [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Compassionate
- Committed
- Impartial
- Respectful
- Collaborative

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity features a logo with stylized human figures in blue, green, orange, and yellow, symbolizing unity and community. The color palette includes Celadon Blue, Orange Peel, and Green Pigment, each representing core values. Typography uses Frutiger LT Arabic in four styles. The brand emphasizes clean, professional layouts, geometric patterns, and consistent use of logo, colors, and fonts across applications [^3].

##### Brand Values

- ## 1.3 Values ### Compassion 2.0 We build our interventions as a response to human despair with the purpose to alleviate pain Our Brand and provide sustainable solutions for local problems. The compassion is the core of humanity and it is assumed as such by our organization since we are first humans and then humanitarians.
- ### Commitment We care for our beneficiaries. Our interventions, although developed in the frame of limited funds due to our local capacity, are aimed at providing long term impact. After project completion, we continue to seek support to operate the infrastructures established. Sometimes, as in the case of many PHC, these are the only medical facilities serving thousands of people in a large geographical area, therefore their operation is vital for the lives of people.
- ### Impartiality We respect all people we work with and for. The beneficiaries are the main motive of our programming. We represent their interests and their voice. As a local organization, we raise their message at the level of UN agencies, donors, partners, and ordinary people to understand their situation and seek to support them. When delivering the aid, we act with respect and kindness as these people already suffered enough. All of us are equal and there is no need to disrespect anyone.
