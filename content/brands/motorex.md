## Brand Guidelines

### 2021

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To enable a strong, uniform brand presence through consistent application of core fonts, elements, and colours, supporting efficient communication and merchandising for MOTOREX [^1].

##### Core Values

- Consistency
- Visibility
- Recognition
- Quality Standards
- Strategic Sponsoring

##### Target Audience

- Distributors, merchandising partners, vehicle sponsors, and retail environments including showrooms, shops, and trade fairs [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Professional
- Reliable
- Striking
- Minimalistic
- Distinctive

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on the MOTOREX logo, corporate green and yellow colors, clean layouts, and strict logo usage rules. The brand is visually striking, minimalistic, and consistent across vehicles, merchandising, POS, and events [^3].
