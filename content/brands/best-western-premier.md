## Brand Guidelines

### 2005

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To maintain Best Western Premier brand identity while allowing each hotel to express its individual personality, ensuring a quality image worldwide and helping travel agents and customers identify hotels that offer additional services and amenities [^1].

##### Core Values

- excellence
- consistency
- quality
- recognition
- individuality

##### Target Audience

- Travel agents, customers seeking premium hotel experiences, and guests who value additional services and amenities [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- prestigious
- authoritative
- professional
- clean
- individualized

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a shield-shaped logo with a dark blue background, yellow border, and a stylized red crown. The logo uses a limited color palette (Pantone 280 Blue, Pantone 116 Yellow, Pantone 485 Red, Pantone 873 Gold), clear typographic hierarchy, and approved typefaces (ITC Century Book Condensed, Adobe Helvetica Neue). Consistent use of logo, color bar, and high-quality materials is required across signage, stationery, brochures, and business cards [^3].
