## Brand Guidelines

### 2020

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To position Hong Kong as 'Asia's World City,' emphasizing its role as a vibrant, multicultural, and dynamic global hub that thrives on diversity, opportunity, and excellence.

##### Core Values

- free
- cosmopolitan
- enterprising
- secure
- excellence
- connected
- innovative
- diverse
- quality living
- dynamic

##### Target Audience

- International business professionals, tourists, students, and global citizens seeking opportunity, cultural diversity, and a modern urban environment.

##### Personality Traits

- dynamic
- innovative
- cosmopolitan
- enterprising
- secure
- connected
- diverse

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity features vibrant, flowing ribbon-like curves and a stylized dragon symbol, using a diverse color palette to convey energy, multiculturalism, and modernity. The logo and graphics are dynamic, colorful, and emphasize Hong Kong's unique fusion of East meets West.

#### Categories

##### Brand Imagery

- The Brand Hong Kong signature comprises of three elements: • The Brand Hong Kong logotype @ The Brandline @ The Dragon symbol with extending ribbons graphic
- All elements are set in fixed format and proportion. They are not to be altered.
- The Dragon symbol with extending ribbons graphic is a unique design that is copyright protected. The Dragon symbol may be used on its own only in exceptional cases.
- The Dragon symbol is a modernised interpretation of a Chinese mythical icon. It represents Hong Kong's unique fusion of East meets West.
- The blue and green ribbons that extend from the Dragon symbolise blue sky and a sustainable environment, while the red ribbon is a silhouette of Lion Rock, which represents Hong Kong people's "can-do" spirit.
- Whenever possible, use the primary version of the Brand Hong Kong signature as shown. The primary signature is the full colour Dragon with extending ribbons graphic, the logotype and brandline.
- The single language version, either English, Traditional Chinese or Simplified Chinese is preferred for primary usage. The bilingual version may be used if the content is entirely bilingual.
- Minimum Size: 40mm
