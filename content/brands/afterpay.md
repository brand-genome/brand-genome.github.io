## Brand Guidelines

### 2020

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To power an economy in which everyone wins [^1].

##### Core Values

- Keep it Real: Genuine, authentic, clear, and sincere [^2].
- Be Brave: Intuition, curiosity, courage, tenacity, grit, teamwork [^3].
- Do the Right Thing: Win/win for stakeholders, accountability, honesty, humility, transparency [^4].
- Shape the Future: Excitement for opportunities, quick thoughtful decisions, intensity, quality [^5].

##### Target Audience

- The 'eyes-wide-open-minded generation'—not defined by age, gender, or color; includes singles, married, parents, diverse backgrounds, values equality, sustainability, accessibility, transparency, educated, discerning, values experiences and nice things, wants freedom and control over finances [^6].

##### Personality Traits

- Optimistic [^7]
- Authentic [^2]

- Responsible [^10]
- Customer-centric [^11]
- Friendly [^12]

##### Visual Identity Overview

- Minimalist, modern, clean design with a focus on bold sans-serif typography (Italian Plate No2 Expanded), a distinctive looped arrow logo symbolizing connection and continuous relationships, a primary color palette of Afterpay Bondi Mint, black, and white, and clear guidelines for logo usage, badge, iconography, and layout to ensure legibility and brand integrity across digital and print applications [^13][^12] .
