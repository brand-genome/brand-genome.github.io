##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a heraldic badge featuring a blue lion holding a staff, surrounded by roses and footballs, with a color palette of blue, gold, red, and white. The badge is used consistently across merchandise, packaging, and promotional materials, with strict guidelines for logo usage, color reproduction, and exclusion zones to maintain brand integrity and recognition.
