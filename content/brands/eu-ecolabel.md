## Brand Guidelines

### 2022

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The EU Ecolabel is the European Union’s official label for environmental excellence. EU Ecolabel products have a reduced environmental impact across multiple stages of the product lifecycle [^1].

##### Core Values

- environmental excellence
- eco-friendliness
- sustainability
- traceability
- European standards

##### Target Audience

- EU Ecolabel license holders, Competent Bodies, applicants, journalists, publishing houses, and other relevant stakeholders [^2]. Consumers seeking products and services with minimized environmental impact [^3].

##### Personality Traits

- authoritative
- eco-conscious
- transparent
- professional
- modern

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a clean, modern logo featuring a stylized green plant, euro symbol, and a circle of blue stars representing the EU. The color palette uses EU Ecolabel green and blue, with strict guidelines for logo style, size, and placement. Packaging and promotional materials emphasize clarity, legibility, and eco-friendly aesthetics [^4].

#### Categories

##### Logo Usage

- The EU Ecolabel logo is protected in primary EU law. The following format must be applied to correctly reproduce the EU Ecolabel logo in colour or in monochrome (black and white):
- → The logo’s style shall not be modified.
- → Other pictures or logos are not allowed to be part of the EU Ecolabel logo.
- → Text written over the EU Ecolabel logo is prohibited.
- → Both the EU Ecolabel logo and the license number text box shall appear on the good or service’s packaging/marketing material.
- The logo should be visible and legible.
- The logo should be no smaller than 10.6 x 10.6 mm.
- → The right to use the EU Ecolabel logo shall not infringe the European Commission’s trademark rights.
- → The previous version of the EU Ecolabel logo shall no longer be used to market the EU Ecolabel.
- → The logo and license number shall only be used during the validity period of the EU Ecolabel license.
- → The logo must only be used with its corresponding license number in relation to the specific good/service for which it has been awarded and/or on its associated promotional material.
- → It is prohibited to associate the logo with non-EU Ecolabel awarded goods/ services or to use it as part of a license holding company’s general profile.
- → If a license holder has more than one product registered under a license, it suffices to indicate the license number only once next to the logo on any associated promotional material.
- → Displaying the EU Ecolabel license number is fundamental for traceability reasons and to convey clear information to the consumer. Only the European Commission, Competent Bodies, and the EU Ecolabel Helpdesk are authorised to use the EU Ecolabel logo without a license number to promote the EU Ecolabel. Therefore, organisations (who are not license holders) wishing to use the logo need to ask prior authorisation from them.
- → The logo background must not be transparent if the background on which it is placed is patterned.
- The EU Ecolabel logo and license number shall be clearly visible, regardless of the type of packaging.
- The EU Ecolabel logo background and license number shall not be transparent, and shall not be presented without its frame.

##### Digital Guidelines

- Information and guidance provided within the Logo Guidelines for offline/print promotional material, publications etc. also apply to online promotional material, such as online catalogues, online product flyers etc.
- All indications on EU Ecolabel logo use outlined throughout this document shall be equally considered for (offline and online) advertising and free promotional sample purposes, in addition to the following specifications:
- EU Ecolabel license information presented in print or online databases: The public ECAT (EU Ecolabel Product Catalogue) lists the goods and services currently awarded with the EU Ecolabel. The information within ECAT is regularly uploaded on the EU Open Data Platform where it is accessible to anyone. The EU Open Data Platform is the central platform for downloading this public data; print or online databases interested in promoting EU Ecolabel licenses for marketing or communication purposes can download the latest data on the EU Ecolabel at any time. All rules that apply to the logo and license number usage also apply to this type of marketing initiative. For further information and clarification of questions etc., please contact the EU Ecolabel Helpdesk.

Please ensure you submit accompanying texts about how the EU Ecolabel is to be displayed within the educational material. All the rules that apply to the style and size of the EU Ecolabel logo as specified in these guidelines shall apply.

##### Multilingual Guidelines

- → The translation of the text “Better for the environment….better for you”, along with all optional and mandatory text translations shall be taken from the EU Ecolabel Regulation EC. No. 66/2010, available in all official EU languages.
- → The optional logo is available in all EU languages. Contact the responsible Competent Body for the colour and monochrome (black and white) modifiable template files.
- Regarding different language versions, only the three below options can be used to communicate the good’s/service’s minimised environmental impact via the optional logo.
- Option 1: Include any desired translations of bullet points into EU languages in one box without translations of: “Better for the environment..., ...better for you”.
- Option 2: Include any desired translations of bullet points into EU languages in one box with the translation of: “Better for the environment..., ...better for you”. in all corresponding EU languages.
- Option 3: Create different boxes for each different EU language translation.
