## Brand Guidelines

### 2015

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To relaunch the nearly bicentennial Araujo & Sobrinho, opening the brand to new opportunities, products, and markets worldwide through rebranding, while promoting the development of the Portuguese fine art industry and creating vintage hand-crafted stationery, furniture, and gifts for studios and clients globally [^1].

##### Core Values

- afirmação
- autenticidade
- continuidade
- família
- história

- património
- redesign
- rebranding

##### Target Audience

- Studios and clients around the world seeking vintage, hand-crafted stationery, furniture, and gifts, as well as those interested in fine art and heritage [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- authentic
- historic

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand's visual identity centers on ornate, vintage emblems featuring a stylized seahorse, intricate floral and scroll motifs, and classic serif typography. The color palette includes rich black, Pantone 288 C, Pantone 8640 C, Pantone 1955 C, and Pantone 8022, with layouts emphasizing elegance, heritage, and craftsmanship [^3].
