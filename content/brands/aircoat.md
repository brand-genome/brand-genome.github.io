## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The AIRCOAT project promotes a ground-breaking passive air lubrication technology with a high potential to revolutionise the ship-coating sector by reducing energy consumption and ship emissions, inspired by the Salvinia effect [^1].

##### Core Values

- eco-friendliness
- innovation
- efficiency
- biomimicry
- sustainability

##### Target Audience

- Organizations and stakeholders in maritime, shipping, and environmental technology sectors seeking innovative, sustainable solutions for ship efficiency and reduced emissions [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- clean
- professional
- minimalistic
- harmonious
- organic
- technological

##### Visual Identity Overview

- AIRCOAT's visual identity is defined by a minimalistic, clean, and modern style, using bold, flat colors and geometric shapes. The logo and graphic elements combine organic and technological motifs, with a preference for rounded shapes, a harmonious color palette, and consistent use of custom typography. The brand imagery emphasizes eco-friendly and maritime themes, with icons and waves as recurring motifs [^3].

- The primary typefaces are Co Text and Co Headlines . Those typefaces are to be used for all AIRCOAT communications.
- Soft and round are the words that best describe the Co font family. Its underlying geometry structures the font design and allows it to have an even rhythm. The harmony between organic and geometric shapes makes Co Headline uniquely appealing for display and titling where special expression is required. Co Text carries even more organic and fluid design elements than its headline cousin. To assist with legibility and functionality, features have been tightened and its basic proportions are less geometric in their appearance.
- When use of Co fonts is not possible, like in emails or Microsoft Office documents, use Verdana or Arial .
- In cases of concerns to the lisibility of the font, Co Text Regular may be used instead of Co Text Light for body text.
- Type: do not change the typography used in the logo.

##### Visual Style

- The AIRCOAT logo highlight the place of air lubrication technology within the project by displaying bubbles in both the icon and the wordmark.
- The green color of the boat refers to the biomimetic hull coating, inspired by the salvinia plant. That coating produces a thin permanent air layer, which reduces friction – increasing speed and reducing fuel consumption – and lessen fouling.
