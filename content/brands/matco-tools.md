## Brand Guidelines

### 2014

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To provide premium quality tools that professionals can rely on to get the job done better and faster with outstanding service, maintaining a commitment to quality, innovation, and partnership with distributors and customers [^1].

##### Core Values

- quality
- professionalism
- competitive spirit
- innovation
- outstanding service
- honesty
- reliability
- partnership
- trust [^2]

##### Target Audience

- Professional mechanics, automotive enthusiasts, institutional customers, and those who value high-quality tools, served through a network of independent franchised mobile distributors and industrial sales representatives [^3].

##### Personality Traits

- down-to-earth
- relatable
- honest
- straightforward
- friendly
- competitive [^2]

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand features a bold, industrial visual style with a distinctive logo (hexagon with red and blue emblem), a strong color palette (black, PMS 293 blue, PMS 1795 red), clean typography, and consistent application across products, packaging, signage, and merchandise. The visual identity emphasizes clarity, strength, and recognizability [^5].
