## Brand Guidelines

### 2011

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The World Water Council aims to promote awareness, build political commitment, and trigger action on critical water issues at all levels, including encouraging sustainable water management and access globally [^1].

##### Core Values

- global cooperation
- sustainability
- access to clean water
- education
- awareness

##### Target Audience

- International stakeholders, policymakers, educators, and organizations involved in water management, conservation, and advocacy [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- professional
- calm
- clean
- minimalistic
- harmonious

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on water imagery, especially stylized blue water droplets, concentric ripple patterns, and gradients of blue. The style is clean, minimalistic, and professional, with consistent use of blue tones (Pantone 640), sans-serif typography (Gill Sans, Humanist 521 BT, Helvetica Condensed), and strict logo usage rules. The brand emphasizes calmness, clarity, and global unity [^3].

#### Categories

##### Brand Imagery

- Concentric Water Ripples - The image should feature a pattern of concentric circular ripples emanating from a central point, depicted with shades of light blue and white to create a sense of depth and movement in water. The ripples are evenly spaced, expanding outward across the frame, and the background is predominantly white with subtle gradient shading to enhance the ripple effect. The overall scene evokes the visual of water disturbed by a droplet or object, emphasizing calmness and fluid dynamics, with a clean and minimalistic style.
- Radiating Circular Waves Pattern - The image should depict a series of concentric circles emanating outward in a spiral pattern, rendered in light blue with subtle shading to create a sense of depth and motion. These circular waves are set against a predominantly white background, with the circles gradually increasing in size as they move from the top left corner to the bottom right, giving the impression of ripples or radiating energy. The overall design is minimalistic, emphasizing smooth, clean lines and a calming, harmonious color palette. The pattern may evoke the idea of sound waves, water ripples, or abstract focus points, with a gentle gradient effect enhancing the visual flow from the central point outward.
- Abstract Water Drop Shape - The image should depict a large, rounded, and bulbous shape resembling a water droplet or a smooth, abstract organic form. It has a gentle gradient fill, starting with a light, almost white hue at the lower left area that seamlessly transitions into darker shades of blue towards the upper right. The shape features a subtle highlight at the top, indicating a light source coming from above and slightly to the left, creating a soft, reflective effect. The overall form is smooth, with no sharp edges or textures, emphasizing a sleek, polished appearance. The background is plain or transparent, accentuating the shape's fluid, organic silhouette.
- Gradient Blue Shape - The image should depict a large, smooth, boat-shaped or semi-elliptical object with a gradient of blue shades. The object has a rounded, convex bottom and a slightly narrower, curved top extending upward, creating an overall organic, flowing form. The color transitions from light blue at the bottom left to darker blue at the top right, suggesting depth or shading. The background is transparent or white, highlighting the shape's fluidity and simplicity. The top edge has a subtle, white, curved highlight or reflective area, indicating a light source from above or the top left. The object appears three-dimensional due to the gradient shading and smooth surface, with no distinct textures or additional elements present.
