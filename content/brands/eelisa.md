## Brand Guidelines

### 2024

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To consolidate successful aspects of EELISA's previous stage and align with strategic focus points as a European University Alliance, aiming to bridge European Engineering, Sciences, and Humanities for inclusive and sustainable societies [^1].

##### Core Values

- Multidisciplinarity
- Diversity
- Building up connections
- Transparency
- Go beyond one-way communication
- Create expanded impact
- Sustainability
- Connectivity
- Commitment

##### Target Audience

- Stakeholders of EELISA, including students, faculty, partner institutions, and European university communities, with a focus on inclusivity and multiculturalism [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Inclusive
- Transparent
- Sustainable
- Connected
- Committed
- Professional
- Modern

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is rooted in engineering, featuring a modular logo system, monochromatic and earthy color palettes, custom typography, and geometric graphic language. It emphasizes clarity, flexibility, and a contemporary, tech-inspired aesthetic, with consistent use of logo, fonts, and iconography across applications [^3].

#### Categories

##### Brand Voice

- The EELISA 2.0 Communication Principles aim to serve as a framework for the different EELISA communication agents and help them align their communication efforts, narratives and messages with the EELISA 2.0
- They encompass aspects such as diversity, transparency, sustainability, connectivity and commitment.
- Embrace DIVERSITY: Multiplicity of voices and formats. Use language and narratives that highlight inclusivity, multiculturalism, and diversity.
- Boost TRANSPARENCY: Prioritize creative content showcasing the EELISA experience.
- Pursue SUSTAINABILITY: Communicate with sustainability in mind, aiming for a zero environmental footprint.
- Promote CONNECTIVITY: Seek out narratives that are respectful and facilitate connecting among.
- Be COMMITTED
- Seek a positive impact in each of your communications actions. The way we craft our stories shapes us
- - From visuals to written language - our communication materials must emphasize inclusiveness and diversity in terms of gender and cultural backgrounds.
- - Highlight the diversity of actors involved in EELISA, and channel the diversity of their voices and points of view.
- - Encourage an open dialogue by following “evolutive communication” formulas that prioritize conversations over unilateral information.
- - Through visuals, graphics. language and social media strategies we will underline the connection between institutions, EELISA communities, research topics and stakeholders.
- - We will acknowledge, share and create content that reinforce the idea of connection between engineering, tech innovation and society.
- - We will emphasize the connected multi-actor, multi-layer and multi-project organizational structure of EELISA instead of a top-down unidirectional communication.
- - Communications will emphasize topics that tackle with environmental, economic, social and ecological sustainability in connection to the European Agenda 2030 and SDGs.
- - We will aim for zero footprint or minimum waste by prioritizing the use of wall projections and video screening of marketing materials in communication campaigns and other activities.
- - We will always use recycled and biodegradable materials for printed marketing materials when possible.
- - Our language and communications should focus on those processes and interactions that lead to transformative dialogues. Such dialogue therefore becomes the raw material of our communication and the starting point of content curation.
- - We will emphasize creative contents that construct a collective vision of EELISA through the production of a wide spectrum of narratives from different points of view and multiple formats.
- - We will aim to build a community that will help expand EELISA´s resources and the scope of its mission – to this extent, each EELISA´s participant is a potential storyteller.
