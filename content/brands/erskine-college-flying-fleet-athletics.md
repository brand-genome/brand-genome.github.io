##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a stylized skull with a wing, primarily in dark maroon and black, conveying speed, freedom, and power. The logo is bold and modern, with clean layouts, clear separation between sections, and a limited color palette of maroon, black, gold, and gray. Typography uses the Univers type family in various weights for titles, headings, and body copy, ensuring legibility and consistency. Logo usage is strictly regulated, with guidelines for sizing, spacing, color backgrounds, and one-color applications [^3].

- The image should feature a stylized graphic of a skull with an abstract design, primarily in dark maroon color, outlined in black.
