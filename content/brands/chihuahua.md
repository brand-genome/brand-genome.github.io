## Brand Guidelines

### 2017

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To provide a unified, consistent, and official visual identity for the Government of the State of Chihuahua, fostering solidarity, justice, humanity, honesty, transparency, and inclusivity across all communications and applications [^1].

##### Core Values

- solidarity
- justice
- humanity
- honesty
- transparency
- plurality
- unity
- equity
- diversity
- loyalty
- valor
- hospitality
- integrity
- effort
- spirituality
- readiness
- regional pride
- heritage

##### Target Audience

- Citizens of Chihuahua, including men, women, youth, children, elders, and people from both rural and urban areas, as well as government officials and institutional stakeholders [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- official
- inclusive
- modern
- approachable
- transparent
- human
- just
- solidary
- honest
- diverse
- unified

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is characterized by a formal, official emblem (shield) and a vibrant, colorful map logo, both emphasizing regional symbolism, unity, diversity, and inclusivity. The design uses bold blue and gray tones, Gotham font family, and strict guidelines for logo usage, color palette, and typography to ensure consistency across all institutional and promotional materials [^3].

##### Brand Values

- Surrounding the shield is an elaborate border with inscriptions reading 'ESTADO DE CHIHUAHUA VALIENTE LEAL Y PIEDOSO,' indicating the state's motto.
- The overall layout is clean with a formal, official appearance suitable for a government emblem, emphasizing symbolism of the region and authority.
- Below the map, the word 'Chihuahua' should be prominently displayed in bold, blue, uppercase and lowercase letters, with the phrase 'AMANECE PARA TODOS' in smaller, gray uppercase letters beneath it.
- The overall composition should be clean and vibrant, with high contrast between the map colors and the text, conveying a lively and dynamic aesthetic.
- Within the layout, beneath or beside the map, there should be the word 'Chihuahua' written in large, bold, blue font, and below that, the phrase 'AMANECER PARA TODOS' in smaller, uppercase, gray font.
- The overall design must be clean, colorful, and modern, emphasizing regional diversity and inclusivity, with the map as the central element and the text positioned to complement it, creating a balanced visual composition.
- Beneath the map, the text 'Chihuahua' should appear prominently in large, bold, deep blue font, capturing attention and indicating the focus on the Chihuahua breed. Below that, in smaller, gray uppercase letters, the phrase 'AMANECE PARA TODOS' should be displayed, serving as a slogan or motto.
- The overall design should be clean and modern, with the map centrally positioned above the text, and the colors vibrant to emphasize diversity and inclusion, consistent with the theme suggested by the slogan.
