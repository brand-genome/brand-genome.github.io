## Brand Guidelines

### 2016

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- CIP delivers innovative science-based solutions to enhance access to affordable nutritious food, foster inclusive sustainable business and employment growth, and drive climate resilience of root and tuber agri-food systems [^1].

##### Core Values

- innovation
- sustainability
- inclusivity
- nutrition
- climate resilience
- global collaboration

##### Target Audience

- Stakeholders in agriculture research, including national and regional research institutes, civil society organizations, academia, development organizations, the private sector, and communities in Africa, Asia, and Latin America [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- scientific
- collaborative
- global
- inclusive
- resilient

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity features a logo with orange and brown colors representing potato and sweetpotato, a stylized figure (Papa Arariwa) holding root crops, and a clean, modern design. The color palette includes warm hues and watercolor textures, with consistent use of Square and Myriad Pro fonts. The brand emphasizes clarity, professionalism, and a harmonious, inviting atmosphere across all communications .
