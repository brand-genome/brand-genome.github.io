## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To represent and promote the Gilgandra Region through a vibrant, modern, and community-driven visual identity that reflects the unique character and collaborative spirit of the area.

##### Core Values

- community
- collaboration
- vibrancy
- regional pride
- inclusivity

##### Target Audience

- Visitors, tourists, and residents interested in rural Australian experiences, regional adventure, and community engagement.

##### Personality Traits

- welcoming
- dynamic
- artistic
- authentic
- adventurous

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses colorful, abstract brushstroke motifs and bold sans-serif typography to convey a lively, artistic, and inclusive regional identity. The logo and visual elements emphasize diversity, collaboration, and the natural landscape, with consistent use of vibrant colors and modern layouts across print and digital media.

#### Categories

##### Color Palette

- It is important the correct colour formats are used at all times.
- Following is an outline of the colour references used in each of the approved logo files.
- The CMYK logo is to be used in all print applications where the 4 colour process is being used.
- For example: Brochures, press adverts or any commercial 4 colour printing.
- The PMS logo is to be used in print mediums where the printing process doesn’t allow the CMYK colour system but does allow PMS colour system.
- For example: embroidery, screen printing, some sign writing.
- The RGB version is to be used on all digital and screen based reproductions.
- For example: Website, Television Commercial, Powerpoint presentations, as well as office based usage, such as in Microsoft Word.
- The Mono logo is to be used on all one colour mediums where tones of a single colour are NOT possible.
- For example: One colour printed material such as faxes and other one colour printing that my be subject to photocopying or low quality reproduction.
- The Gilgandra Region primary logo is not to be used in the following ways. This will also apply to the Gilgandra Region word mark logo.
- VIVID COLOURED BACKGROUND
- STRETCHED OR CONDENSED
- ON BUSY PATTERN OR IMAGE
- CROPPED
- ALTERED OR ADJUSTED
- CHANGE OF COLOUR

##### Typography

- When a set of fonts are used consistently in communications, it further strengthens the brands recognition.
- Below are our suite of brand fonts that compliment one another and offer contrast and flexibility for the range of communication material the brand will require.
- MUSEO 300, 500, 700 This is our heading font to be used for the headings in our communications. The bold version (700) is used to highlight a specfic word.
- Avenir Light, Light Oblique, Book, Book Oblique, Medium, Medium Oblique, Black, Black Oblique This is our body font to be used for the body copy/large areas of text in our communications. The Black version is used to highlight a specfic word.
- Rogan Winters This is our feature font to be used for the region taglines. This should only be used in lowercase.
