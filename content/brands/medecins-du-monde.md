## Brand Guidelines

### 2022

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Médecins du Monde is an independent movement of active militants, in France and internationally, who provide care, bear witness, and support social change. Their mission is to enable excluded people and their communities to access health, while fighting for universal access to care and advocating for health as a fundamental right [^1].

##### Core Values

- solidarity
- justice
- inclusion
- advocacy
- universal access to healthcare
- respect for human rights
- militancy
- independence

##### Target Audience

- Populations most vulnerable and excluded from healthcare, including homeless people, drug users, migrants, asylum seekers, squat residents, incarcerated individuals, isolated minors, sex workers, and residents of sensitive areas [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- militant
- dynamic
- strong
- inclusive
- compassionate
- professional
- advocacy-driven

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is characterized by a strong, dynamic, and militant style inspired by activist posters. It uses bold typography (Anton Regular, Open Sans), a minimalistic and high-contrast color palette (navy blue, sky blue, orange, white), and prominent logo usage featuring a circular emblem with a dove and medical cross. Layouts are clean, professional, and emphasize clarity, with full-bleed photography and strict print guidelines. The brand consistently applies its logo, slogan, and colors across all print and digital materials [^3].
