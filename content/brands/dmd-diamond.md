## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- DMD Diamond aims to empower people to achieve financial freedom by making every DMD coin an interest bearing asset with high annual interest rate. DMD Diamond is achieving this goal by creating a great network architecture, ever evolving software and sensible management.

##### Core Values

- innovation
- integrity
- focus
- scarcity
- value
- security

##### Target Audience

- Individuals seeking a modern, secure, and efficient digital currency for global transactions, wealth storage, and investment, particularly those interested in blockchain technology and financial freedom.

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- professional
- innovative
- secure
- refined

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is clean, modern, and minimalistic, featuring a stylized geometric diamond logo, a consistent blue and white color palette, and sans-serif typography (Arial and Gotham Rounded). The brand emphasizes clarity, professionalism, and high-tech aesthetics across all visual elements.
