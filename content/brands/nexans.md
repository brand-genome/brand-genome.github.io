## Brand Guidelines

### 2023

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Nexans is on a mission toward sustainable electrification, combining innovation, value, and responsibility to bring light where it needs to be. The company aims to electrify the future with impact, balancing profit and sobriety through its E3 pillars: Environment, Economy, and Engagement [^1].

##### Core Values

- Environment
- Economy
- Engagement
- Reliability
- Innovation
- Performance
- Social Inclusion
- Safety
- Solidarity
- Transparency
- Trust
- Dedication
- Unity
- Pioneering

##### Target Audience

- Industries and individuals across the electrification value chain, including engineering, design, installation, maintenance, recycling, repair, and repurposing. Nexans also addresses customers in global, regional, and local markets, and is committed to social inclusion and the electrification of developing countries [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Inspiring
- Specific
- True
- Direct
- Questioning
- Impulse Creator
- Optimistic
- Attentive
- Dedicated
- Pioneering
- United

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is modern, striking, and minimalistic, emphasizing clarity and impact. It features bold red (red gold/copper) and black as primary colors, with gradients and abstract curves symbolizing energy and connectivity. The logo is a stylized 'N' with a wave-like design, and the visual style is consistent across applications, favoring readability and adaptability to various backgrounds. Typography is structured with Nohemi, Jost, and Arial fonts for different uses [^3].

##### Typography

- To maintain a consistent and identifiable image on all media, Nexans must use only three families of characters.
- Nohemi typography is used to structure the content. It is used in specific cases: - Titles and subtitles, - Quotes, - Key figures.
- The second typography is the Jost, which fits perfectly with the Nohemi and is used for writing everyday text, in digital and office media.
- The Jost typography is free of right and accessible to all.
- The third typeface is Arial, which is used when Jost is not applicable.
