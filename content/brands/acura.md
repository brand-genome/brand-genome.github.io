## Brand Guidelines

### 2021

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Acura is a premium performance brand. The guidelines are designed to maintain a unified brand identity for Acura, emphasizing Precision Crafted Performance across all communications and touchpoints [^1].

##### Core Values

- performance
- precision
- innovation
- consistency
- independent spirit

##### Target Audience

- Consumers seeking premium, high-performance vehicles with a modern, bold, and innovative identity [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- bold
- intelligent
- provocative
- innovative
- confident
- optimistic

##### Visual Identity Overview

- Acura's visual identity is defined by a distinctive logo, bold modern color palette, performance-inspired typography, and dynamic photography. The brand uses clean layouts, high contrast, and minimalistic design elements to communicate speed, performance, and sophistication [^3].

##### Typography

- Futura Now For Acura Condensed Extra Black is our primary headline font. It was chosen to reflect the qualities of the brand personality and visually complement its content and tone of voice. Futura Now For Acura Condensed Extra Black Italic is primarily used in Acura Motorsports pieces.
- Futura Now For Acura Regular and Medium are primarily used for legal copy. All other weights of Futura Now For Acura are used on a case-by-case basis and must be approved by Acura Marketing.
- The Futura Now For Acura font family is published by Monotype and can be purchased here: https://www.monotype.com/fonts/futura-now
- Acura Bespoke is the primary text typeface. It complements the bold performance personality that Futura Now For Acura Condensed Extra Black has, bringing an intelligent, provocative, innovative visual presence to the brand. The font is available in a number of weights, allowing ease of use and flexibility while maintaining consistency.
- The Acura Bespoke typeface is available on Acura AdBuilder.
- Acura Performance is a custom typeface based on the signature model badges of the full line. It communicates movement and the feel of the road while reintroducing a hint of racing nostalgia. Acura Performance should be used sparingly. It should not be used for headlines. It is a secondary typeface to be used as a graphic element.
- Avenir is the primary typeface used on Acura web properties. Using a mix of Futura Now For Acura Condensed Extra Black for headlines and Avenir for body copy and CTAs, our typography will remain consistent with that of brand communications.
- The Avenir font family is published by Linotype and can be purchased here: https://www.linotype.com/72/avenir-family.html
- When using our typefaces together, a clear hierarchy should be followed. Futura Now For Acura Condensed Extra Black should always be used for headlines. It’s used in all caps, large, and graphically to bring the tone of voice to life. If a subhead follows, it should be set in Acura Bespoke Regular in sentence case.
- All body and text copy should be set in Acura Bespoke Regular as well. Depending on the size of the medium, Acura Bespoke Book may be used, as long as it is legible.
- Included on this page are the recommended settings for kerning, tracking, and word spacing to ensure that headlines in Futura Now For Acura Condensed Extra Black remain legible and bold; however, kerning will need hand-finessing to achieve the best outcome.
- Headlines are set with Roman Hanging Punctuation, i.e., the text is centered minus the period. The period should be added after the words are centered.
- Kerning is set at Optical.
- Tracking is set at -30 pt. Leading is 90% of the font size.
- Punctuation: Scaled down to 85%.
- Word Spacing: Minimum: 70% Desired: 75% Maximum: 100%
- Use the above specs as a starting point. Each headline must be manually kerned so the letters are tight and uniform in spacing.
- Below are examples of how and how not to use our typography. Acura may appear in all caps in headlines, text, and branding. It may not be used in the Acura Performance font, as it becomes too stylized. Futura Now For Acura Condensed Extra Black is the primary headline font. The Acura Bespoke and Acura Performance fonts may not be used for headlines unless otherwise approved. Drop shadows and unapproved colors should not be used on any Acura fonts. For legibility purposes, photos should be retouched. Futura Now For Acura Condensed Extra Black may be used for long copy, but we recommend not using it in sizes smaller than 20 pt. Do not use the Acura Performance font for text copy.
