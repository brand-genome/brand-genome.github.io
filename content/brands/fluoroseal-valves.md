## Brand Guidelines

### 2021

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To work in partnership with our customers to help them achieve their goals by providing a full range of standard and customized industrial valves, manufactured to the highest quality standards and delivered for the best possible value. We constantly innovate and improve, creating durable, reliable, low-emissions valves. Our vision is to be a world-leader in the creation of innovative, low-emissions industrial valves that help clients reduce their environmental impact [^1].

##### Core Values

- Quality
- Customer Commitment
- Innovation
- Integrity
- Responsibility
- Legacy

##### Target Audience

- Clients in the oil and gas, chemical, petrochemical, mining, and water industries, as well as other sectors requiring high-quality industrial valves [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Personal
- Knowledgeable
- Respectful
- Professional
- Open
- Enthusiastic
- Pioneering
- Collaborative

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity consists of a predominantly blue and white color palette, a modern and fluid logo, strong and personal Lato typography, consistent iconography, and imagery that highlights high-quality products, technical environments, and authentic human interaction. The style is minimalistic, professional, and emphasizes clarity, technical precision, and brand consistency across all applications [^3].
