## Brand Guidelines

### 2011

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Corban University strives to send clear messages—written and visual—to our external audiences. The university aims to develop a consistent and positive brand message, accurately portraying Corban University as an institution that offers a high-quality, Christ-centered education to its students. The logo is inspired by Corban’s mission to educate Christians to make a difference in the world for Jesus Christ [^1].

##### Core Values

- Christ-centered education
- Relationship (student to student and faculty to student)
- Dedication of heart and mind to God
- High-quality academic standards
- Global mission
- Judeo/Christian history
- Learning

##### Target Audience

- External audiences including prospective students, families, and the broader community seeking a high-quality, Christ-centered education [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Trustworthy
- Stable
- Traditional
- Professional
- Positive
- Consistent

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a shield-shaped emblem with a divided color scheme (orange and navy blue) and a prominent white cross, conveying stability, trust, and tradition. The logo uses Visage serif typeface for the university name, with a formal and institutional appearance. The color palette includes navy blue, gold yellow, gray, and white. The visual style emphasizes balance, clarity, and a Christ-centered message [^3].

#### Categories

##### Brand Imagery

- The Corban University logo is the officially recognized marketing symbol of the university. It is inspired by Corban’s mission to educate Christians to make a difference in the world for Jesus Christ.
- The components of the logo are a shield containing the stylized letters C and U. The path through the middle of the shield represents the journey of all Christians through life and also completes the cross that represents Christ. The university name is set in Visage typeface. The logo can be used either stacked or horizontal, with or without motto (see pg. 8).
- Any publication intended for viewing by an audience outside Corban University must display the logo with attention to the guidelines in this document.
- The university logo, seal (see pg. 12) and athletic marks (see pg. 13) are the only marks approved for official university business conducted with internal and external audiences.
- When a school-specific logo is needed, the name of the school replaces the word University in the logo in Visage small caps as shown. Do not attempt to recreate these logos by replacing University with the school name. The typeface is specifically designed for each school.
- The stacked version of the logo is preferred in this format.
- Aspect ration of symbol and text should not be changed (Hint: hold down the shift key).
- Do not attempt to retype the words in the logo or use a different typeface. The Visage typeface is part of the logo. Do not add additional text to the logo for a department or event. For department or event specific logos, please contact the Office of Marketing & Communications.
- The elements of the logo, the shield and typeface, should not be separated or transposed. In special circumstances, by permission only, the shield may be used by itself apart from the typeface. The typeface should never be used alone.
- Do not make the logo too large or too small. The logo should be no larger than 25% (width and/or height) of the Too Small page space and no smaller than 1.5" wide for the horizontal logo and I" wide for the stacked logo.
- When using the shield as a design element, apart from the typeface to complement a layout, it can be made larger or smaller than the specified measurements above. The shield may only be used by itself under special circumstances with permission and approval from the Office of Marketing & Communications.
