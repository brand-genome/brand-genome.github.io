#### Brand Summary

- The document does not explicitly state a mission or purpose statement for NAHREP, but it emphasizes the importance of controlled use of logo and colors to communicate the strengths of the organization [^1].

##### Target Audience

- The target audience is not explicitly described, but the guide is for those creating print and digital materials for the National Association of Hispanic Real Estate Professionals [^2].

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is characterized by a consistent use of the NAHREP logo, a specific color palette (including bright lime green, light teal, coral red, navy blue, and gray), and preferred typography (Bebas Neue, Gotham, Futura). The brand uses abstract, colorful circles and clean, minimalistic layouts to convey vibrancy and professionalism [^3].

##### Logo Usage

- Our logo is the constant that is used in every communication, either digital or printed. The logo should be considered as a pictoral mark, and not a combination of text and graphic elements. Accordingly, all margins, fonts and other elements must not be modified in any way. Accent logo shall only be against the main NAHREP blue, in any other instace where a dark background is required, use the full white logo. Black logo is to only be used when a color print is not available.
- Mark is only to be used by NAHREP National in cases such as social media icons, social postings, opp icons, and other digital collateral. For print collateral, mark must be positioned alongside any reference to NAHREP's full name.
- The size of the logo plays an important role in how our brand communicates with the audience in the way that it was originally intended. The NAHREP logo must always be legible and maintain the integrity of its form and colors. For light backgrounds, the full color version must have 20% black tint or less. If the background is 21% black or more, use the white version of the logo.
