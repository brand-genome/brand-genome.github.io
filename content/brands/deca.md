## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- DECA prepares emerging leaders and entrepreneurs in marketing, finance, hospitality and management.

##### Core Values

- Competence
- Innovation
- Integrity
- Teamwork

##### Target Audience

- High school and college students around the globe, including members, educators, school administrators, and business professionals.

##### Personality Traits

- Ambitious
- High-achieving
- Community oriented
- Professionally responsible
- Experienced leaders

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is clean, modern, and professional, emphasizing simplicity, clarity, and boldness. It features geometric shapes, primarily diamond or cube motifs, a solid blue color palette, and bold, uppercase sans-serif typography. The logo is used in both horizontal and vertical formats, with strict guidelines for clear space, sizing, and color usage to maintain brand integrity.

##### Brand Imagery

- Clear space frames the logo, seperating it from other elements such as text, imagery and headlines. Clear space that is a minimum of half the height of the E in the logo should be provided on all sides.
- In order to maintain legibility and impact, the DECA logo should not appear below 0.5 inches for on screen and print. There is no maximum size limit.
