## Brand Guidelines

### 2020

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The Defense Health Agency (DHA) is a joint, integrated Combat Support Agency that enables the Army, Navy, and Air Force medical services to provide a medically ready force and ready medical force to Combatant Commands in both peacetime and wartime. The DHA supports the delivery of integrated, affordable, and high quality health services to Military Health System (MHS) beneficiaries and is responsible for driving greater integration of clinical and business processes across the MHS. DHA manages TRICARE, the nation’s largest health benefit plan.

##### Core Values

- integration
- affordability
- high quality health services
- readiness
- trust

##### Target Audience

- Military Health System beneficiaries, DHA staff, staff at military hospitals and clinics, their support organizations, contractor partners, and licensees.

##### Personality Traits

- authority
- trustworthiness
- professionalism
- modernity

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The DHA visual identity is clean, modern, and professional, emphasizing authority and trustworthiness. The logo features bold navy blue letters, a white star, and wavy stripes reminiscent of the U.S. flag, with a color palette of gray plus one accent color. Typography uses ITC Franklin Gothic for headers and Minion Pro for body text, ensuring consistency and legibility. The logo and seal are protected trademarks and must be used according to strict guidelines.

#### Categories

##### Typography

- The correct use of typefaces is essential to maintain the MHS brand identity by adding consistency and continuity. For DHA, this includes the typefaces ITC Franklin Gothic and Minion Pro. Both typefaces include a wide variety of styles and weights that can be used in different arrangements to give DHA publications a consistent and professional appearance.
- Adhere closely to these guidelines when using DHA typefaces:
- ⌘ Do not use special effects, such as drop shadow, that compromise legibility.
- ⌘ Do not distort the typefaces (for example, expand, condense or modify the letterforms).
- ⌘ Do not substitute typefaces.
- ITC Franklin Gothic, a sans serif font designed to be highly legible at a distance and in small text sizes, is the proposed primary typeface for headers and subheaders.
- Minion Pro, a classic serif font, is the proposed primary typeface for body text. Its partially condensed style saves space, making it adaptable to different layouts and well-suited for copy.
