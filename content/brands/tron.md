## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To build an effective, consistent, and distinctive image of the FILTRON brand, ensuring precise and reliable filtration and long-term market success through consistent brand communication and high-quality products.

##### Core Values

- precision
- reliability
- trustworthiness
- consistency
- quality

##### Target Audience

- Mechanics and professionals in the automotive industry across Europe, particularly those seeking high-quality filtration products.

##### Personality Traits

- technological
- precise
- trustworthy
- professional
- consistent

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is based on bright metallic greys, modular elements, legible typography, and consistent logo usage. Packaging uses distinct colors for product groups, and pictograms are used as characteristic elements. The brand emphasizes clarity, technical aesthetics, and high-quality graphics.
