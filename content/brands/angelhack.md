## Brand Guidelines

### 2013

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- AngelHack organizes hackathon competitions for web developers and entrepreneurs, providing opportunities to learn developer tools, meet new friends, and receive funding and mentorship for startup ideas. Winners participate in the AngelHack Accelerator Program, culminating in introductions to Silicon Valley investors and incubators [^1].

##### Core Values

- innovation
- community
- mentorship
- entrepreneurship

##### Target Audience

- Web developers and entrepreneurs seeking opportunities to learn, network, and launch startup ideas through hackathons and accelerator programs [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- innovative
- professional
- minimalistic
- community-focused

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is clean and minimalistic, featuring a bold red color palette, Helvetica Neue typography, and a stylized crown icon. The logo is used with strict guidelines for background and color, and iconography is consistent with red backgrounds and white symbols [^3].

#### Categories

##### Logo Usage

- Our logo is the touchstone of our brand and one of our most valuable assets. We must ensure proper usage.
- The preferred way to use the AngelHack logo is over a white/light background. Every attempt must be made to do this.
- If the logo must be placed on a dark background, the AngelHack logo can be knocked out and used in white color.
- Don't: 1. Change logo's orientation. 2. Add extraneous effects to the logo. 3. Place the logo on busy photography. 4. Change the logo colors. 5. Attempt to recreate the logo. 6. Scale the logo unproportionately.
