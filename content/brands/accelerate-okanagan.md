## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To give new and growing technology-driven businesses the mentorship, connections, and the community they need to thrive.

##### Core Values

- Inclusive
- Community focused
- Resourceful
- Smart
- Fun
- Passionate
- Collaborative
- Leaders
- Coaches

##### Target Audience

- Entrepreneurs and technology-driven businesses at various stages, including startups, growth-stage companies, and alumni. The audience includes founders seeking mentorship, community, and support, as well as supporters, sponsors, and institutions within the Okanagan tech ecosystem.

##### Personality Traits

- Relatable
- Fun
- Smart

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity emphasizes bold, modern, and minimalistic designs with geometric shapes, predominantly red tones, and clean layouts. The logo features a stylized network/molecule graphic and bold typography. Brand imagery includes architectural photos, people connecting, and duotone images using brand colors. Graphics and iconography are consistent across programs, with circles and network motifs representing community and connectivity.
