## Brand Guidelines

### 2017

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To consistently execute our mission with integrity, clear and honest communication, and a commitment to safety, earning the trust and reliance of our customers while building meaningful relationships and delivering outstanding service in aviation.

##### Core Values

- integrity
- clear communication
- safety
- excellence
- ambition
- customer-centricity
- relationship-building

##### Target Audience

- Clients and customers seeking reliable, safe, and tailored aviation services, including corporate, charter, and government sectors.

##### Personality Traits

- ambitious
- trustworthy
- professional
- customer-focused
- passionate
- unified
- resolute

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses a modern, geometric, and abstract visual style dominated by shades of blue and white, with clean lines, bold sans-serif typography, and a signature logo mark featuring a compass rose and the word 'CONTOUR'. Imagery is photo-driven, emphasizing aircraft and aviation operations, with strict guidelines on authenticity and no embellishments.

#### Categories

##### Brand Voice

- Fueled by passion. Powered by people.
- Contour is a diverse company united by a love of aviation. In 1982, we were two employees and one airplane. Today, we are more than 300 team members and 30+ airplanes.
- Our integrity is never conditional. We work hard to earn the trust of our customers through clear and honest communication, and a commitment to safety above all else, but we aim higher. Clients do more than just trust us; they rely on us. The consistency with which we flawlessly execute our mission is at the heart of the Contour brand.
- We build meaningful relationships with each and every client, because to understand a customer you have to think like one. We take the time to design solutions tailored to their individual needs.
- The Contour team is unified in our commitment to outstanding service on the ground, in the air and behind the scenes of our operation. We believe excellence is an attitude; an approach that serves as the lens through which we see every interaction and decision.
- Our principles are resolute. Our future is limitless.
- Contour is ambition in flight.

##### Brand Imagery

- With the exception of materials specific to Contour Airlines, we take a photo-driven approach. High resolution wide-angle photography is used to draw attention to the size and scope of our operations. Alternatively, close-in photography can be used to accentuate the fine details of aircraft and other aviation-related concepts in a more abstract manner. Stock photos of aircraft not currently in the Contour fleet are not permitted. Please contact the Contour marketing department to obtain photos of specific aircraft.
- Imagery should always be true to the product and/or service being provided. Embellishments, such as, lavish catering, limousines and race cars should never be used.
- For Contour Airlines, we used illustration as an extension on our brand. As a rule, we do not use pictures of aircraft or destinations in the marketing of Contour Airlines. The graphics are designed to highlight ticket prices and/or support awareness of the intangible aspects of our product: convenience, reliability and the friendly nature of Contour service.
