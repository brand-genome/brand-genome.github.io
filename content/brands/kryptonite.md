#### Brand Summary

- To ensure you can go where you want, when you want, by offering smart, serious security solutions and safe lighting solutions. Kryptonite aims to be the portable security standard against which all others are compared, providing unparalleled product quality and service with integrity [^1].

- Unparalleled Quality
- Legendary Service
- Experience
- Never Finished. Always Evolving

##### Target Audience

- Cyclists, powersport users, and anyone seeking portable security solutions, including dealers, end users, law enforcement, and those who value safety and security [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Trusted Advisor
- Innovative
- Integrity-driven
- Responsive
- Technical

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is simple and technical, aligning with safety and security categories. The wordmark is 100% black, with preferred color combinations of black on yellow, and strict rules against using green. Typography uses United Sans Semi-Condensed Heavy for headlines and Stratum for body text. The logo requires clear space and must not be altered, with strict guidelines for color, size, and usage [^3].

- The ad layout is structured around an underlaying grid system. These are just guidelines, the exact dimensions of the grid, gutter, margins, etc are flexible based on size of the final ad.
- Kryptonite branding is placed on a black "tab" that comes in from the edge of the page and can overlap photos and other design elements. This is supposed to function almost like a sticker or tag and should not be perfectly aligned with the grid system.
- Equal margins on all sides. Here they are .5", but they can be adjusted based on the size of the ad.
- Within the border is a 6x12 grid with 12pt gutters. The main lifestyle photos and content should be arranged within this grid structure. Photos should have the gutter width maintained between them. They should not be touching.
- Pencil elements are used for supplementary emphasis on key aspects of the design, especially around text and framing details.
- Clipped product photography is placed directly on the textured yellow background with a very light drop shadow to help it stand out. These elements are loosely placed based on the structural grid, but should be adjusted so that they are generally horizontally centered within the yellow space.
