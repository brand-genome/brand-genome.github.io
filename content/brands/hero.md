## Brand Guidelines

### 2013

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The Hero brand aims to communicate engineering, intelligence, and a warmer, more human element through its modern abstract icon and uniquely crafted wordmark [^1].

##### Core Values

- engineering
- intelligence
- clarity
- warmth
- human element

##### Target Audience

- Not explicitly stated in the guidelines.

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- confident
- assured
- friendly
- dynamic

##### Visual Identity Overview

- Hero's visual identity centers on geometric shapes, a bold red and black color palette, a modern sans-serif wordmark, and strict logo usage rules. The design is clean, minimalistic, and emphasizes clarity, legibility, and visual impact [^2].

##### Typography

- The Wordmark is a uniquely crafted modern typeface. The combination of clean lines and open letterforms adds a warmer more human element to the Brand identity.
- The primary English typeface is TStar. It is modern and solid whilst communicating with confidence and assurance. TStar's distinctive letterforms offer gravitas at larger sizes whilst retaining simplicity and legibility at smaller sizes. TStar should be used predominantly on all English communication.
