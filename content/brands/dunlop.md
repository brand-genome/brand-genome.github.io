## Brand Guidelines

### 2024

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- No explicit mission statement is provided in the document.

##### Target Audience

- The guidelines and logo usage are focused on motorcycle-related events and products, indicating the target audience is motorcycle enthusiasts, riders, and motorsports participants [^1].

##### Personality Traits

- dynamic
- strong
- professional
- speed-oriented

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on bold, italicized typography, a distinctive 'Flying D' logo element, and a color palette of bright yellow, red, black, and gray. Logos are used consistently across backgrounds, with strict rules for color combinations and placement. The brand emphasizes clarity, motion, and high contrast in its visual presentation [^2].

- If logo is on a colorful or busy background, include the yellow rectangle.

- "Flying D" element must always point to the right. It may never be rotated.
