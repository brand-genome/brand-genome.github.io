## Brand Guidelines

### 2022

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To promote relevant and quality higher education in the country by maintaining consistency in communications and materials produced by the agency, supporting nation-building and educational reform.

##### Core Values

- consistency
- transparency
- excellence
- nation-building
- educational reform

##### Target Audience

- All CHED Central and Regional Offices, stakeholders in higher education, and the general public engaging with official communications and materials.

##### Personality Traits

- authoritative
- professional
- transparent
- modern
- unified

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a circular emblem featuring a sunburst, pyramid, and human silhouette, using Royal Blue, Crimson Red, Golden Yellow, White, and Rich Black. The logo is applied consistently across materials, with clear rules for color, typography, layout, and usage, reflecting authority, enlightenment, and progress.

##### Typography

- The Commission’s name is represented by Georgia Regular encircling the mark, while the subhead is in Helvetica LT STD Bold, both in Rich Black.
- The agency’s full, abbreviated, and regional offices’ names are represented by Georgia Regular in Rich Black, both in horizontal and vertical orientation.
- Fira Sans is the Commission’s official primary typeface, and Georgia is the official secondary typeface for all CHED communication materials to be posted and published in the official websites and social media accounts.
- The primary typeface for official correspondences such as letters, memoranda, and issuances is Arial, 11 pt.
- The Fira Sans typeface is the official typeface for all official communication materials. Fira Sans is a humanist sans-serif typeface that aims to cover the legibility needs for a large range of handsets varying in screen quality and rendering.
- Fira Sans Condensed and Fira Sans Compressed are also available, if needed.
- The Georgia Font has been selected for use as secondary typeface for communication materials.
- Body texts should be in Arial at 11 pt.
- Body texts should be in Fira Sans.
- All other texts are in Georgia at 11 pt
- Headline texts should be in Fira Sans Font.
- Body texts should be in Helvetica.
