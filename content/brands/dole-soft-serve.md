## Brand Guidelines

### 2020

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To help operators make a fruitful profit from our better-for-you treats and to promise quality and a positive experience to consumers and retailers at every point of contact.

##### Core Values

- quality
- better-for-you
- consistency
- approachability
- fun

##### Target Audience

- Foodservice operators, consumers seeking better-for-you frozen treats, and retailers in the U.S.

##### Personality Traits

- fun
- approachable
- true-to-fruit
- on-trend

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity features vibrant, playful colors, a signature logo with a sunburst and stylized text, pastel and tropical imagery, and consistent use of the Lato typeface. The brand emphasizes fresh, colorful visuals and clear logo usage guidelines.

#### Categories

##### Brand Voice

- DOLE SOFT SERVE® is a true-to-fruit frozen treat with on-trend attributes that consumers seek.
- APPROVED DESCRIPTIONS: Fruit flavored (or mango-flavored, pineapple-flavored, etc.), Vegan, Dairy-free, Lactose-free, Gluten-free, Cholesterol-free, Fat-free, Made without artificial colors, Convenient (for operators), DOLE SOFT SERVE®, Better-for-you, No artificial color
- UNACCEPTABLE DESCRIPTIONS: With, contains, or made with real or fresh fruit, Natural or All Natural Ingredients, Organic, Made without artificial flavors, Ready-to-use (RTU), Disney® DOLE Whip®, Original DOLE WHIP®, DOLE Whip®, Healthy, Natural color
- Never refer to or use Disney or its properties, which are trademarked, in connection to DOLE Whip®
- Never refer to DOLE Whip®

##### Brand Imagery

- Perfect, tropical-colored swirls can be shown in cones or dishes, either with or without toppings.
- Fresh fruit may be used as a garnish or a topping, provided it is clear the soft serve itself doesn't contain real fruit.
- Backgrounds and environments should be light, airy, and neutral to showcase the colors of DOLE SOFT SERVE®.
- In order to maintain color consistency when showing images of DOLE SOFT SERVE® in cones or cups, the following color guide has been created to help you match the color of the images to that of the finished product.

##### Logo Usage

- A key element to reinforce brand recognition and trust, the DOLE SOFT SERVE® signature must be applied consistently for all marketing collateral. The signature artwork should never be redrawn or altered in any way.
- The full-color Dole word mark paired—or "locked up"—with the stylized SOFT SERVE text. This should be used whenever possible.
- Official Name: DOLE SOFT SERVE®
- The logo should always appear on white or light screen of less than 5%
- To maintain the logo's integrity, keep a minimum amount of clear space around its perimeter. This space is determined by at least the height of the lowercase "L'' in any proportion the logo is used. The DOLE SOFT SERVE® logo can't be smaller than 1.25" (31.8mm).
- The DOLE SOFT SERVE® logo should be displayed consistently in every circumstance. Below are instances which take the trademark out of acceptable and consistent usage. ALWAYS use the logo in its intended form.
- Do not stretch
- Do not rotate
- Do not change the colors
- Do not change the inner leaf color
- Do not reposition the Dole wordmark
- Do not remove the shadow
- Do not reposition the shadow
- Do not separate the wordmark or use the elements individually
- No use of the DOLE marks on printed advertising (ie. Post cards, flyers etc.), permanent signage, billboards, commercials, store front, store names, labeling, packaging, shipping containers or other materials, business documents, invoices, stationery, sweepstakes, drawings, giveaways, labels, cartons, or permanent wrapping that has not been previously approved in writing by Dole Packaged Foods.
