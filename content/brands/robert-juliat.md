## Brand Guidelines

### 2012

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The communications strategy of ROBERT JULIAT exists to strengthen its image, broadcast its good reputation and ensure the brand remains instantly recognizable across a wide professional public. This begins with a higher control of the distribution of its visual identity [^1].

##### Core Values

- Uncompromising quality
- Consistency
- Professionalism
- Elegance
- Dynamism
- Modernity

##### Target Audience

- A wide professional public, including printers, graphic designers, and anyone in charge of company-related work such as posters, announcements, adverts, and online media [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Elegant
- Dynamic
- Modern
- Professional
- Consistent

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is centered around the 'RJ' symbol and a distinctive typography, with strict guidelines for logo usage, color palette, and typography to ensure cohesion and recognizability. The brand uses a minimalistic, modern style with a focus on black, white, grey, and accent colors, and applies its standards across all media and stationery [^3].

##### Typography

- The Vandiana Platin Lite font is the new typography for the ROBERT JULIAT's logo since 2009. It brings elegance, dynamism and modernity. No other font can be used for the logo.
- This font is also used for all on-line and off-line publications, without its transformation* (brochures, documentations, etc.). It is applied only on the head titles.
- For letters and the internal applications (business letters, e-mails, etc.), Arial is the standard font. For multimedia (Intranet, PowerPoint, Internet), Vandiana, Myriad Pro and/or Arial fonts are used.
- On certain value-added documents to emphasize on creativity and design, other fonts can be used to give rhythm to texts. Two sans serif fonts, Formata (the same family as Myriad Pro) and Verdana, are frequently used (catalogs, documentations, etc.).
- By default, due to any technical problems generated when processing documents with software, the Arial font can be used in substitution.
- Only the "ROBERT JULIAT" typography in the logo and the product names has a 12° angle with an outline to bring a more dynamic effect.
