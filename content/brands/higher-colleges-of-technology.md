#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The document does not explicitly state a mission or purpose statement for the brand.

##### Target Audience

- The target audience is not directly described, but the context and institutional branding suggest it is prospective and current students, faculty, and stakeholders of the Higher Colleges of Technology.

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is characterized by a stylized eagle emblem within an arched shield, using bold, continuous lines and a color palette of green, black, red, and white. The logo is used in various alignments and language combinations, emphasizing institutional professionalism, clarity, and national symbolism. Typography guidelines specify Helvetica Neue LT Std for English and AXI Manal for Arabic, with strict logo usage and color palette rules for consistency across applications.
