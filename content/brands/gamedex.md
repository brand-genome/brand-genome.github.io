## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Digital collectible card powered by games blockchain. From instantly-verifiable authenticity to cryptographically-limited editions, blockchain technology stands to revolutionize collectibles. The next generation of fantastical creatures, digital pets, and sports cards is coming soon.

##### Core Values

- authenticity
- innovation
- security
- modernity

##### Target Audience

- Individuals interested in digital collectibles, blockchain technology, gaming, and next-generation digital pets and sports cards.

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- bold
- innovative
- clean

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand features a geometric, stylized logo mark, bold sans-serif typography, and a modern color palette with strong contrasts. Visuals emphasize clarity, simplicity, and a professional, minimalistic style, with repeatable patterns and consistent logo lockups across various backgrounds.

#### Categories

- GAMEDEX LOGO LOCKUP SPACING RULE When the logo lockup is used, the padding should be 2x of the logo mark outline.
- When the logo mark is used independently, the padding should be 2x of the logo mark outline.
- The logo mark can be freely used to create a repeatable, identifiable pattern on any surface or digital space.
