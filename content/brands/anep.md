##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is formal, clean, and professional, featuring a blue and white color palette, a stylized emblem with a sun, shield, laurel wreath, and bold uppercase typography. The logo and layouts emphasize clarity, hierarchy, and institutional authority, with consistent use of vertical lines and spacing to separate elements. Typography is specified as black or white depending on background, and the main font is 'Fieldwork Hum Demibold' in uppercase for the ANEP logotype. The brand architecture is structured to visually distinguish different organizational branches while maintaining coherence with the overall identity.

##### Color Palette

- The image should feature a logo on the left side consisting of a circular emblem with a blue and white color scheme. Inside the circle, there is a stylized sun with yellow rays extending outward at the top. The emblem contains detailed imagery including a shield with a cross, a horse, a tree, a mountain, and a wave pattern, all in blue and white. Surrounding the shield are olive branches in white, forming a laurel wreath that encircles the emblem. To the right of the emblem, there is a vertical blue line separating it from the text. Next to the line, the text 'ANEP' is displayed prominently in large, bold, white uppercase letters, on a light blue background.
