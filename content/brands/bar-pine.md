## Brand Guidelines

### 2022

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To provide a space to be and belong, fostering creativity, culture, and community through a reflective cocktail experience and recreation of small communities and networks [^1].

##### Core Values

- Individualized
- Reflective
- Responsible
- Art
- Cultural Progress
- Elegance
- Sophistication
- Discretion
- Inspiration
- Incubation
- Fermentation
- Rebellion

##### Target Audience

- Creatives and like-minded individuals seeking a relaxing and sensible refuge, cultural key players, and those interested in art, culture, and elegant sophistication [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Elegant
- Sophisticated
- Rebellious
- Discreet
- Reflective
- Inspirational
- Responsible
- Minimalist

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on minimalist, modern aesthetics with a strong emphasis on the pine tree motif, using stylized Chinese characters and clean, sans-serif Latin fonts. The color palette is monochrome (black, white, deep gray) with strict guidelines for logo usage, clear space, and adaptability across backgrounds. Typography is carefully selected to reflect the brand's personality, and iconography is minimal and geometric, supporting a refined, contemporary visual style [^3].
