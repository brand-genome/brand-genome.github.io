##### Visual Identity Overview

- Minimalist, modern, and bold visual style with strong use of yellow, black, and white. The logo combines the letter 'B' and a 360-degree circle, emphasizing completeness and business focus. Iconography and layouts are clean, geometric, and digital-oriented, with consistent use of branded colors and stylized typography.
