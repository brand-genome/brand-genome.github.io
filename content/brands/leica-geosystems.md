## Brand Guidelines

### 2014

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To provide reliable measurement and surveying solutions, emphasizing precision and quality for professionals who require accurate spatial information. The tagline 'when it has to be right' encapsulates the brand's commitment to dependability and excellence in geospatial technology [^1].

##### Core Values

- precision
- quality
- dependability
- service
- global consistency
- trusted results

##### Target Audience

- Professional surveyors, engineers, and business partners (distributors and service partners) who operate in measurement, surveying, and geospatial industries, requiring high-quality, reliable instruments and support [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- professional
- trustworthy
- innovative
- global
- consistent

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is defined by the prominent use of the Leica Geosystems logo in bold red (Pantone 185C/U) on a white background, with strict rules for logo usage, proportions, and color palette. The brand employs clean, minimalistic layouts, technical imagery, and consistent application of logos and taglines across all materials. The font Signa Column is used for typography, and visual elements emphasize clarity, professionalism, and technical expertise [^3].
