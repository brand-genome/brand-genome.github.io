## Brand Guidelines

### 2019

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- FileWave brand identity represents the values, services, ideas, and personality of our organization. It is designed to increase recognition and build perceptions of FileWave in its chosen marketplace [^1].

##### Core Values

- recognition
- integrity
- consistency
- innovation
- simplicity
- professionalism

##### Target Audience

- The intended audience includes industries such as government, K12 education, higher education, and enterprise IT [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- dynamic
- simple
- expressive
- professional

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a modern, sleek logo with a bold, lowercase wordmark and a dynamic red swoosh icon. The color palette features primary FileWave blue and red, with secondary colors for flexibility. Typography is contemporary and simple, using Mr Eaves XL Mod OT for print and Open Sans for digital. Iconography is high-contrast and uses secondary colors. Photography is expressive, natural, and eye-catching, supporting technology themes [^3].
