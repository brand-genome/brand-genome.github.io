## Brand Guidelines

### 2020

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The Biden for President campaign is a scrappy, grassroots movement ready to restore the soul of the nation, aiming to lead a united campaign and a united country.

##### Core Values

- unity
- grassroots engagement
- restoring the soul of the nation
- pride in America
- building a better America

##### Target Audience

- Supporters of Joe Biden and individuals who share the vision for a united America, including those who wish to show their #TeamJoe pride using campaign design assets.

##### Personality Traits

- scrappy
- grassroots
- prideful
- united
- visionary

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity features a clean and professional layout, bold uppercase logo typography, a color palette including Biden Blue, Honest Red, Shell White, Joe Navy, and Coral of the Year, and the official campaign font Brother 1816. The brand uses multiple logos and design assets to represent its vision for America, with clear guidelines for logo usage and color specifications.
