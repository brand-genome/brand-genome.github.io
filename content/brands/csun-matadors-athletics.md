## Brand Guidelines

### 2014

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To ensure consistent use of the athletic department’s logos, symbols, color and typography across a wide variety of applications, representing CSUN Matadors Athletics visually and maintaining the integrity of its brand identity.

##### Core Values

- strength
- tradition
- consistency
- team pride

##### Target Audience

- Internal departments and external vendors who apply the University’s marks, as well as the broader community engaging with CSUN Athletics.

##### Personality Traits

- bold
- confident
- energetic
- commanding

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on stylized matador figures, bold block typography, a strong red, black, and white color palette, and consistent logo usage. The style is clean, sharp, and dynamic, emphasizing movement, strength, and tradition.
