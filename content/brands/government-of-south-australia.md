## Brand Guidelines

### 2024

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To ensure a consistent and professional image for the Government of South Australia, enabling recognition of government activities, information, and services by all levels of the community, and fostering collaboration across departments for the benefit of the public [^1].

##### Core Values

- integrity
- consistency
- collaboration
- professionalism
- public service

##### Target Audience

- All levels of the community in South Australia, including public sector employees, government entities, and the general public [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- authoritative
- formal
- systematic
- clear
- collaborative

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is minimalistic, formal, and systematic, featuring a single identifiable logo (roundel with the Piping Shrike and 'Government of South Australia'), prescribed color palette (blue, red, yellow, charcoal grey, black), professional typography (Frutiger LT Std), and strict guidelines for logo usage, layout, and supporting graphics to maintain clarity and authority [^3].

##### Visual Style

- The background color should be a solid dark blue. The title text should be aligned towards the upper left portion of the cover in bold, white, uppercase letters, with each line separated by a thin, horizontal white line. The text should include 'Government of South Australia', then a line break, 'Branding Guidelines', then another line break, and 'April 2024' at the bottom of the text block, all left-aligned. The bottom right corner should contain a circular logo representing the Government of South Australia, with a simple, official emblem — potentially featuring a stylized graphic or official insignia — rendered in monochrome, with the words 'Government of South Australia' encircling the emblem. The logo should be partially overlaid on a white triangular shape that extends from the bottom right edge diagonally towards the center, creating a clean, modern contrast. The overall layout should be minimalistic, with a clear hierarchy of information, professional typography, and balanced spacing to convey authority and clarity. The image as a whole should evoke formality, officialdom, and systematic branding consistent with government document cover pages.

##### Signage Guidelines

- Signs that only state the department, branch or unit name are not required to include an approved logo. Examples include, internal and external building signs, which tell the visitor their location.
- Should the logo be used, it must be in proportion to the design to which it is applied.
- Signage that is specifically designed to impart information or promote a particular activity should include an approved entity logo. Examples include: walking trail signs, infrastructure development notice signs, etc.
- When in mono, the GOSA logo should be reproduced in Black, PMS 295 Blue or White reverse only.
- Where a co-branding exemption has been granted, both the GOSA and entity logo should be produced in equal visual weight.
- The logo size must be in proportion to the design to which it is applied.
