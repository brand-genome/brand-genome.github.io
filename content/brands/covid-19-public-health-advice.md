## Brand Guidelines

### 2020

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To protect and enhance the integrity of the COVID-19 identity, ensuring consistency and accurate use for public recognition and visibility in all health-related communications and collateral [^1].

##### Core Values

- Consistency
- Accuracy
- Visibility
- Public Health
- Compliance

##### Target Audience

- Government agencies, third parties, and the general public seeking official COVID-19 public health advice and information [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Official
- Clear
- Serious
- Professional
- Minimalistic

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is defined by a signature yellow color palette, bold and legible typography (Lato and Arial), strict logo usage rules, clear zones, minimum sizes, and consistent iconography. The design is minimalistic, with high contrast and clarity, emphasizing official public health messaging [^3].

#### Categories

##### Brand Imagery

- The COVID-19 ‘Public Health Advice’ logo is used on all health related collateral. The logo comprises of a symbol and the name ‘Coronavirus (COVID-19) Public Health Advice’. The symbol and text are integral elements of the logo and cannot be separated. The relationship between these elements is fixed and cannot be altered in any way. The logo only appears in black and white. No other colours can be used. Master artwork is available for English and Irish logos.
- Logo when it appears on the signature yellow background. NOTE the logo where possible should appear on its signature yellow background on all collateral.

- To ensure the logo is clearly reproduced a minimum size has been specified. The minimum size is determined by the height of the logo which is 12mm. See example below.
