## Brand Guidelines

### 2019

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Providing prevention, intervention training, and support for those affected by suicide. Supporting emotional well-being in young people and those affected by suicide [^1].

##### Core Values

- care
- share
- support
- prevention
- intervention
- empathy

##### Target Audience

- Young people and individuals affected by suicide, as well as those seeking emotional well-being support [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- empathetic
- supportive
- caring
- open
- inclusive

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses a consistent green and white color palette, clean and bold typography (Poppins SemiBold and Regular), and a suite of logos and icons with clear space and minimum size guidelines. Visuals emphasize clarity, simplicity, and emotional support, with print and digital adaptations [^3].

#### Categories

##### Logo Usage

- The full colour logos are the primary versions of our logos and should be used whenever possible. The mono versions of our logos are used only for black and white publications when the full colour logos cannot be reproduced. The white versions of our logos are for use on dark and coloured backgrounds.
- An area of clear space should be left around the logo to ensure maximum clarity. The clear space is dictated by the size of the size of the 'u'. As this logo hangs off the top edge of materials, a clear space is not needed at the top of the logo.
- An area of clear space should be left around the icon to ensure maximum clarity. The clear space is dictated by the size of the size of the inside tail of the 'u'.
- An area of clear space should be left around the logotype to ensure maximum clarity. The clear space is dictated by the size of the size of the 'u'.
- Minimum size guidelines are established to ensure that our logo and icon are legible at all times.
- There are separate versions of each logo available for print and digital use. CMYK versions are prepared for print and RGB versions are for digital use. There are multiple file formats, such as EPS, PNG and SVG available for both colour options.
- The full logo should be used when its it can sit top left and hang off the edge of the asset.
- The icon logo should be used when space is limited and where displaying the full name is not crucial.
- The logotype can be used when the logo cannot hang off the top edge, or if the 'u' has been used elsewhere as a device.
