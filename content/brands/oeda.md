## Brand Guidelines

### 2017

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To provide direction for the Ohio Economic Development Association's brand identity, emphasizing real-world impact through its members, partners, and staff, and fostering a living, breathing brand that adapts and evolves with input from its community [^1].

##### Core Values

- community
- collaboration
- modernity
- clarity
- effectiveness

##### Target Audience

- Members, partners, staff, and potential members of the Ohio Economic Development Association, as well as stakeholders in economic development within Ohio [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- refined
- highly legible
- energetic
- businesslike
- minimalistic

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is defined by a modern, geometric logo with concentric circles and a wordmark in Brandon Grotesque, a sunrise-inspired color palette, clear typographic hierarchy, minimalistic and sharp imagery, and structured grid-based layouts. Iconography follows strict sizing and proportional rules, and all visual elements are designed for clarity, harmony, and effectiveness [^3].

##### Layout And Composition

- OEDA‘s corporate marks have been designed upon an underlying grid to ensure proper uniformity – they have a fixed relationship that should never be changed in any way. The base unit for the underlying grid is equal to the width of one band of concentric lines, or the square area of crossover within the E and D in the wordmark.
- It is important to keep corporate marks clear of any other graphic elements. To regulate this, an exclusion zone has been established around the each element, as well as each mark as a whole.
