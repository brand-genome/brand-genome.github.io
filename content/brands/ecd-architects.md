## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- ECD Architects aims to deliver high quality architectural and building consultancy services focused on improving the environment we all live and work in, with an emphasis on energy conscious design and sustainability [^1].

##### Core Values

- We care
- We achieve as a team
- We improve
- We deliver

##### Target Audience

- Clients seeking sustainable, low-energy, and high-quality architectural solutions, including local authorities, government departments, housing associations, developers, charitable trusts, public and private companies [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Professional
- Collaborative
- Innovative
- Caring
- Quality-driven

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is minimalistic and professional, using a primary palette of greens and greys, a modern sans-serif typeface (FS Albert), and a logo featuring a stylized leaf and wind motif. The brand emphasizes clarity, sustainability, and consistency across all materials [^3].

##### Logo Usage

- The ECD logo is the backbone of our identity.
- It should be treated with respect and never distorted. It is essential that all items use the original artwork files to help preserve the logo's integrity.
- To preserve the integrity of the Company name, and avoid confusion, the company strap line Energy Conscious Design is never to appear without either the company name or logo.
- There is a single colour version of the logo for use against a white background.
- The full colour version of the logo uses the ECD greens and ECD grey to give the logo maximum impact.
- The logo can also be reversed out on the ECD primary green or black.
- The logo should always be reproduced using the correct original artwork, and applied carefully within these guidelines.
- The logo should always be re-sized in proportion and never be distorted in any way from its original dimensions.
- The logo should never be cropped, or the symbol and typography separated.
- The Logo should never be reproduced in different colours.
- The logo should wherever possible be positioned at the top left of the page. If this is not possible, the logo should be moved to the bottom left.
- Where possible the logo should appear in colour.
