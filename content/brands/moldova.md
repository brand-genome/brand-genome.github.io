## Brand Guidelines

### 2019

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To position Moldova as a place where any idea, initiative, and investment comes to life and bears fruit, emphasizing growth, unity, and development rooted in tradition and innovation [^1].

##### Core Values

- growth
- unity
- tradition
- innovation
- hospitality
- authenticity
- development
- connectivity
- cultural heritage
- welcoming spirit
- diversity

##### Target Audience

- Investors, tourists, and international partners seeking authentic experiences, cultural richness, and opportunities in a modern, connected, and hospitable environment [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- welcoming
- generous
- authentic
- innovative
- connected
- vibrant
- professional
- clear
- modern

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a stylized tree of life motif, combining vibrant, interconnected geometric shapes and symbols representing growth, connectivity, tradition, and modernity. The color palette is diverse and bold, with multicolored branches and icons, and the logo is adaptable for various backgrounds and applications. Ornamentation and visual style draw from folk art and technological motifs, creating a unique blend of heritage and innovation [^3].
