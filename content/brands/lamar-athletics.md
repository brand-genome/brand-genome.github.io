## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To ensure consistent and appropriate use of Lamar University Athletics' logos and visual identity, effectively communicating its unique identity to all key constituents and building upon the tradition and pride associated with LU Athletics [^1].

##### Core Values

- tradition
- pride
- consistency
- flexibility
- recognition

##### Target Audience

- All members of Lamar University, vendors who work with the University, and key constituents of the Athletics Department [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- bold
- fierce
- dynamic
- energetic
- strong

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on bold, angular mascot imagery (primarily a stylized eagle or cardinal head), a vibrant color palette (Lamar Red, White, Black, Gold, Green), and strong, clean typography. Logos are used in various color variations and must adhere to strict usage guidelines to maintain brand integrity [^3].
