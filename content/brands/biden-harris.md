## Brand Guidelines

### 2020

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To lead a united campaign and a united country, fighting in the battle for the soul of the nation, and to help Joe Biden become the 46th President of the United States [^1].

##### Core Values

- unity
- grassroots movement
- fighting for the soul of the nation

##### Target Audience

- Supporters of Joe Biden for President, including those who wish to show their #TeamJoe pride using design assets [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- scrappy
- grassroots
- united

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity features bold, uppercase logos with a stylized red 'E', a versatile color palette of blues, reds, and off-white, and strong, modern typefaces (Decimal and Mercury). Consistency in logo usage, clear space, and color combinations is emphasized, with assets designed for both print and digital applications [^3].
