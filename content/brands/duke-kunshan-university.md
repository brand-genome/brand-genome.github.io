## Brand Guidelines

### 2014

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Duke Kunshan University aims to be an open integration platform for the community, fostering outstanding talents and committing to the harmony of people and nature. The brand is positioned as a unique, ownable identity reflecting the trinity cooperation of Duke University, Wuhan University, and the city government of Kunshan, and is dedicated to passing on knowledge and inspiring creation [^1].

##### Core Values

- integration
- cooperation
- progress
- harmony with nature
- knowledge
- creation
- community contribution

##### Target Audience

- Prospective students, academic professionals, and the broader community seeking a modern, international, and collaborative educational environment [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- young
- futuristic
- inherited
- excellent
- pioneering
- innovative
- elite
- trusted

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is defined by a geometric, three-dimensional triangle logo symbolizing growth, stability, and cooperation, with a color palette of blue, green, and white. The brand uses InterstatePlus for English and Hiragino Sans GB for Chinese typography, vivid imagery to express brand personality, and a supergraphic derived from the logo's triangle, integrating Chinese landscape painting for a modern yet culturally rooted visual style [^3].

##### Supergraphic

- The supergraphic of Duke Kunshan University is a geometric refined form developed by taking the basic triangle from the logo and integrating the essence of Chinese landscape painting. The permutation and combination of this geometric form is brand's modern Supergraphic with Chinese cultural heritage.
- Supergraphic is specially designed and must be replicated from the digital final artwork without changing the form.
- The Supergraphic is composed of two rows of triangle-formed permutations. A single row can be used under special circumstances. It can be used alone or with pictures in the design layout. It's usually applied on brand communication materials, including printing ads, outdoor advertising, magazine advertising, poster, brand brochures, conference backdrops, presentations, handbag and so on.
- The Supergraphic Pattern is composed of rows of Supergraphic , and is the extension of the Supergraphic. It's typically used as background, can be used alone or with the Supergraphic.
- Note: 1. the green isn't the same in Supergraphic and Supergraphic Pattern and should not be mixed.
- 2. The Supergraphic and Supergraphic Pattern can be only rotated 90 degrees clockwise when they need to be used vertically or rotated, and can't be rotated freely or symmetrically.
- Unlimited extension or contraction can be made to the left and right end as needed.
- The minimum size can't be less than the minimum size indicated on this page. Any size smaller, it will be difficult to identify. Under some specific printing conditions, such as newsprint, bronzing printing and screen printing, the size should be relatively larger.
- When displayed on screen, the minimum size can't be less than the minimum size indicated on this page in order to achieve visibility.
- It should be cut to the center of the triangle by both ends when the Supergraphic is cropped.
- In order to meet special layout design situation, the left end of Supergraphic should be cropped to the triangle center when used horizontally and the top end should be cropped to the triangel center when used vertically.
- Only brand blue and green should be used in Supergraphic.

- Two rows of triangles with same brand blue or brand green color can be used according to specific requirement.
- When vertically used, brand blue on the left, brand green on the right, and can't be the reversed way.
- When Supergraphic is used on pictures, its color should be set to white, with 60% transparency.
