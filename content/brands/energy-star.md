## Brand Guidelines

### 2010

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- ENERGY STAR's mission is to protect the environment for future generations by promoting energy efficiency through a partnership among government, business, and consumers [^1].

##### Core Values

- Environmental protection
- Energy efficiency
- Partnership
- Credibility
- Consumer empowerment

##### Target Audience

- Businesses, organizations, and consumers who are interested in energy-efficient products, homes, and buildings, and who value environmental responsibility and cost savings [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Credible
- Modern
- Clear
- Positive
- Motivational

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is clean, modern, and emphasizes energy, brightness, and excellence. It uses a predominantly bright blue color (ENERGY STAR Blue, 100% Cyan), a flowing cursive script for 'energy', a bold star outline, and a strong, clear logo lockup. The design is consistent across applications, with strict guidelines for logo usage, color, and clear space. Imagery is positive, active, and often features sky, individuals, and family situations to reinforce environmental and emotional themes [^3].

- Imagery is a small but very important part of the ENERGY STAR identity.
