## Brand Guidelines

### 2025

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To provide a consistent, professional, and instantly recognizable brand identity for the Mustang Cup, reflecting Ford's racing heritage, innovation, and performance, and ensuring all teams and drivers apply the brand accurately and consistently in every application [^1].

##### Core Values

- heritage
- innovation
- performance
- consistency
- professionalism

##### Target Audience

- Motorsport teams, drivers, and enthusiasts participating in or following the Mustang Cup series, with a focus on those who value high-performance racing and brand integrity [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- dynamic
- competitive
- energetic
- modern
- sleek

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on bold, modern logo designs featuring stylized horse imagery, racing stripes, and a color palette of Ford Blue, Performance Red, Light Gray, and White. The style emphasizes speed, movement, and clarity, with strict rules for logo usage, color contrast, and typography to ensure brand consistency across all applications [^3].
