## Brand Guidelines

### 2021

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The main goal of Nina Bakery is that everyone can enjoy quality bread at a reasonable price. Nina Bakery uses recipes that have been passed down from generation to generation so that the quality and taste of the bread from Nina Bakery is no longer in doubt.

##### Core Values

- handmade
- no preservatives
- quality
- tradition
- professionalism

##### Target Audience

- People seeking quality bread, cakes, and cookies at a reasonable price, with an appreciation for traditional recipes and artisanal methods.

##### Personality Traits

- friendly
- serious
- enthusiastic
- energetic
- integrity

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses handwritten-style typography to convey friendliness and personality, strong letters to show seriousness, and a circular logo with orange as a symbol of energy and enthusiasm. The visual style emphasizes simplicity, warmth, and artisanal quality, with consistent use of color and logo variations for digital and physical applications.
