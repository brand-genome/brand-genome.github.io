## Brand Guidelines

### 2017

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To expedite the world’s transition to clean and affordable hydrogen energy, available to all, by developing safe, affordable, and sustainable solutions for clean energy and water that address pressing global challenges through disruptive technologies and broad partnerships.

##### Core Values

- Inventive
- Purposeful
- Trustworthy
- Optimistic
- Energetic

##### Target Audience

- Industry partners, government organizations, customers, and communities globally, with a special focus on both developed and developing economies seeking clean, affordable energy and water solutions.

##### Personality Traits

- Socially responsible
- Innovative
- Simple
- Friendly
- Unique

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a modern, scientific, and approachable look, featuring a bold logo with rounded lowercase letters, a color palette inspired by hydrogen’s emission spectrum (blues, black, grey, white), Gotham Rounded typography, impactful photography with clean and energetic imagery, and a consistent use of circular and molecular motifs to convey innovation, humanity, and balance.

##### Logo Usage

- Our logo is our most important brand element. It’s our corporate brand name and the primary focus of our marketing and communications materials. With few exceptions, everything we do from a communications standpoint should carry the Joi Scientific logo.
- The Joi Scientific logo combines an ambigram icon with a typographical element. The Joi ambigram can be read from multiple points of view.
- In the early days, we expect these two elements to be present together at all times. As our company develops, it is foreseeable that the ambigram may be used separately in certain branding situations (similar to the Nike swoosh symbol).
- Together, the Joi Scientific logo and tagline form our corporate signature. They are key elements of our marketing and communication materials, and their correct use is important to our success. We have a guidelines document that sets out the rules of usage in more detail.
- The text of our corporate logo is set in a unique Bespoke typeface, which conveys the singular breakthrough that we have made. The Bespoke typeface should never be used, except in this logo incarnation.
