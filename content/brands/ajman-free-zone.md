##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is clean, modern, and minimalistic, featuring a primary color palette of black, white, and silver, with bold geometric logo elements and contemporary typography. Secondary colors are used energetically to highlight information and differentiate sectors. The visual language includes kinetic and super graphic elements derived from the brand symbol, enhancing corporate collateral and expressing a progressive nature.
