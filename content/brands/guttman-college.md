## Brand Guidelines

### 2016

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Stella and Charles Guttman Community College offers a new approach to community college education, linking rigorous academic programs to practical career experiences and is wholly committed to student success through structured, supportive environments and individualized attention [^1].

##### Core Values

- student success
- individual achievement
- educational opportunity
- community engagement
- practical learning

##### Target Audience

- Students seeking a structured, supportive, and practical education in New York City, including those interested in liberal arts, sciences, and information technology [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- purposeful
- engaged
- supportive
- innovative
- community-oriented

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a shield logo divided into colorful segments, with consistent use of blue, orange, yellow, and other secondary colors. The brand employs serif and sans-serif typefaces, clear logo placement rules, and a unified, professional style across all applications [^3].

##### Typography

- The Guttman Community College has a serif and a sans-serif typeface.
- The serif typeface is Chronicle. It can be used for both body copy and headlines. The college logo is typeset in Chronicle small caps. It should be substituted with Georgia on digital publishing platforms.
