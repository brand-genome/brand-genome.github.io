## Brand Guidelines

### 2024

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Lonestar Electric Supply is a local, independent wholesale distributor hyperfocused on creating value through unique and innovative solutions. The company aims to empower employees to MAKE IT HAPPEN and keep decision making close to customers.

##### Core Values

- innovation
- flexibility
- empowerment
- customer-centricity

##### Target Audience

- Customers seeking electric supply solutions, with a focus on local and independent service. Also includes employees and vendors under the Lonestar umbrella.

##### Personality Traits

- dynamic
- flexible
- empowering
- innovative

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses a stylized lightning bolt and star motif, with a color palette inspired by the Texas flag (red, dark blue, white, and an accent blue). Typography centers on the Trade Gothic LT font family, and logo usage is strictly defined with primary and alternate versions for different contexts. Visuals emphasize energy, power, and Texan identity.
