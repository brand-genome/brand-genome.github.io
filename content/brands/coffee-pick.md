## Brand Guidelines

### 2025

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Coffee Pik aims to offer a coffee experience that reflects the spirit of Dubai—modern, vibrant, and rooted in tradition—by blending the rich traditions of Arabic coffee culture with modern coffee brewing techniques and connecting old-world charm with the fast-paced lifestyle of the city.

##### Core Values

- quality
- sustainability
- community
- tradition
- modernity

##### Target Audience

- Individuals seeking a high-quality, locally roasted coffee experience that combines Arabic coffee rituals with contemporary urban lifestyles, including both traditional coffee purists and adventurous drink seekers.

##### Personality Traits

- warm
- inviting
- meticulous
- approachable
- refined
- playful

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity features a blend of traditional Arabic elements and modern aesthetics, including a distinctive Arabian man mascot, playful cartoon illustrations, earthy and vibrant color palettes, elegant typography, and cozy, welcoming imagery. The brand pattern incorporates coffee-related icons and Arabian cultural motifs, ensuring a memorable and cohesive look across merchandise, stationery, and digital platforms.

The green symbolizes freshness, growth, and sustainability, while the yellow brings warmth and energy, ensuring our brand feels both classic and modern. This harmonious mix ensures flexibility while keeping the Coffee Pik identity bold and memorable across all platforms.

##### Typography

- For Coffee Pik, we’ve chosen Playfair Display as the main heading font, offering a touch of classic elegance and sophistication. Its refined serifs and graceful curves evoke a timeless, premium feel, perfect for making a strong statement in headings and titles.
- Complementing this is Raleway as the secondary text font. Its clean, modern lines provide a sleek contrast, ensuring readability and a contemporary edge. Together, these fonts strike the perfect balance between tradition and modernity, enhancing the Coffee Pik brand’s approachable yet refined aesthetic across all media.

##### Logo Usage

- The Coffee Pik logo features a distinctive Arabian man mascot symbolizing tradition, wisdom, and a passion for coffee. With a magnifier over one eye, the mascot is shown closely observing a cup of coffee, embodying our meticulous attention to detail and commitment to crafting the perfect brew. His thoughtful expression reflects the care and precision we pour into every cup, blending the richness of Arabian heritage with modern coffee culture.
- This is our primary logo, complemented by a variant where Coffee Pik is written in both Arabic and English, reinforcing our cultural roots while ensuring a modern and inclusive identity. Together, these elements make Coffee Pik not just a cafe but a destination where coffee is an experience to be explored and enjoyed.
- Our logo variations are designed to maintain the integrity of the Coffee Pik brand across different platforms and media. Whether it's the full logo with both Arabic and English text. simplified wordmarks, or symbolic elements, each version ensures consistent recognition while offering flexibility for print. digital. and advertising applications. These adaptable designs ensure that Coffee Pik's identity shines through, no matter the context.
