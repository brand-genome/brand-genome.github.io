## Brand Guidelines

### 2019

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Our mission is to make sure that all of your travel-related experiences result in perfect moments, always.

##### Core Values

- customized travel experience
- support throughout the travel process
- mutually beneficial relationships
- exploration
- adaptation
- cost savings
- common sense thinking

##### Target Audience

- Travelers, Travel Arrangers, Travel Managers, and Executives who must travel for work and seek Zen-like, common sense-driven business travel experiences.

##### Personality Traits

- visionary
- supportive
- adaptable
- professional
- common sense
- relaxed
- happy

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity features a professional and clean aesthetic with a focus on red, white, and gray color palettes, dynamic wavy line motifs, bold serif and sans-serif typography, and a consistent, modern logo applied with strict usage rules to ensure clarity and brand distinction.
