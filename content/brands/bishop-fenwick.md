## Brand Guidelines

### 2019

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To reflect an all-around standard of excellence in academics, athletics, and all other programs valued by the school, reinforcing the core essence of school pride, accomplishment, and spirit.

##### Core Values

- excellence
- pride
- accomplishment
- spirit

##### Target Audience

- Students, faculty, staff, and the broader school community involved in academics, athletics, and other school programs.

##### Personality Traits

- bold
- energetic
- aggressive
- dynamic
- proud

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on bold, dynamic logos featuring stylized eagle and falcon imagery, a vibrant color palette of red, yellow, white, and black, and custom typography. The style is emblematic, aggressive, and suitable for sports teams and school branding, emphasizing strength, pride, and unity.
