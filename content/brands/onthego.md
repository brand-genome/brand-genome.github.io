## Brand Guidelines

### 2016

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To ensure a successful and consistent ONTHEGO brand by providing guidelines for the correct usage of brand elements and maintaining brand consistency across custom products, corporate collateral, marketing materials, artwork, and retail and custom garments [^1].

##### Core Values

- Consistency
- Simplicity
- Brand Protection
- Approval Process

##### Target Audience

- Clients, partners, and users involved in designing, producing, or marketing custom products, corporate collateral, and branded garments for ONTHEGO SPORTS [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Active
- Dynamic
- Playful
- Energetic
- Casual

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is characterized by a playful, handwritten logo with curved arrows symbolizing movement and activity, a vibrant green hero color, clear guidelines for logo and icon usage, a consistent color palette, and the use of Roboto Condensed as the primary typeface. The brand emphasizes simplicity, clarity, and energetic visual elements suitable for active lifestyles [^3].

#### Categories

##### Brand Imagery

- The core 0NTHEG0® brand elements including logos, graphic elements and colours should not be altered in any way. The following guidelines cover the use of the above within the application of corporate collateral, marketing materials, artwork, and retail and custom garments.
- The OTG Corporate Logo includes the Registered Trademark symbol (®).
- Where applicable use the 'Primary' Logo first, followed by 'Secondary'.
- The 'Black' and 'Reverse' logos are for use on coloured backgrounds or over images.
- The ONTHEGO Logo and Icon must always meet minimum size and spacing guidelines. Under no circumstances is the logo to be obstructed by another logo, image or graphic element.
- The clear space for the Logo is to be calculated by the vertical height of the 'N' in the Logo.
- The clear space for the Icon is calculated by half the size of the Icon {50% proportionally).
- The clear space for both Logo + Icon is to be applied evenly to all four sides.
- DO NOT use the ONTHEGO logo in any colours other that those specified in this guide.
- DO NOT apply an outline to ONTHEGO logo.
- DO NOT skew or rotate the ONTHEGO logo to any position other than specified.
- DO NOT skew or rotate the ONTHEGO icon to any position other than specified.
- DO NOT apply an drop shadow effect to the ONTHEGO logo.
- DO NOT apply an drop shadow effect to the ONTHEGO icon.

##### Typography

- BRAND TYPOGRAPHY
- The Roboto Condensed type family is appropriate for both web, screen and print applications. When used for long copy the typeface is 'Regular' and in Sentence Case.
- Roboto Condensed has a variety of weights and treatments that can be used to provide emphasis. Body text should be in OTG Gray or Black.
- TEXT CAN APPEAR ANY OF THE CORE BRAND COLOURS
- Roboto Condensed is ONTHEGO SPORTS primary type family and should be used whenever possible to communicate brand messages.
- Roboto Condensed is a web sate font, appropriate for both screen and print applications.
- Its is available in multiple weights and treatments -however its use should be limited to 'Regular' + 'Bold'.
- Roboto Condensed 'Bold' in UPPERCASE should be used for all headings and sub-headings.
- Roboto Condensed 'Regular' in Sentence Case should be used for all long copy.
- On rare occasions when the primary typeface is unavailable the alternate type family Lato can be used.
