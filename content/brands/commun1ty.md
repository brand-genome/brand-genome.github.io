## Brand Guidelines

### 2022

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Commun1ty is a marketplace organized around 'groups' focusing on group buy activities led by a host. It’s a social media platform designed around neighbourhood group buys, connecting people through loving communities.

##### Core Values

- connection
- community
- sharing
- neighbourhood
- interest

##### Target Audience

- Individuals interested in group buying activities within their neighbourhoods, seeking social connections and community engagement.

##### Personality Traits

- friendly
- approachable
- modern
- inclusive
- community-oriented

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity features a vibrant color palette with purple as the primary color, accented by teal, yellow, and other secondary colors. The logo combines a heart-shaped icon with interconnected lines and a modern, bold, lowercase logotype. The style is minimalistic, clean, and emphasizes connectivity and friendliness. Iconography includes heart, map, and share symbols, and the typography uses Chivo Black for highlights, Raleway and Roboto for general use.

##### Typography

- We suggest to use Chivo Black only for highlighting main elements (around 5% totally). Raleway and Roboto (definitely for numbers) would be mostly used across the app.
- Since the letters in the word Commun1ty include a number, they do not kern well in all typefaces. For the brand communication (presentations, print material etc) we suggest to use the type family “Chivo” and always write the brand name in All Caps.
