### 2019

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Norske Skog aims to achieve and deliver on its vision, values, goals, and strategies by placing great importance on market perception and stakeholder reputation. The updated identity is designed to present a consistent public image and build a clear reputation, supporting innovation and engagement in green and circular economies alongside established paper production [^1].

##### Target Audience

- Stakeholders, customers, and partners in the paper production and green/circular economy sectors, including subsidiaries, mills, hubs, and business units [^2].

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity features a well-known and conspicuous logo—a stylized green tree—supported by a consistent color palette (green and blue), clear space rules, and guidelines for logo usage. Typography includes Source Sans Pro and Bookerly, with alternatives for internal use. The brand employs graphic elements, icons, and a defined visual tone for imagery, ensuring a unified and professional appearance across all materials [^3].

The commercialisation of products will also require a definite corporate branding strategy.
- Norske Skog relies heavily on the distinctiveness of its corporate name, logo, brand, its various brand names and any other mark, design or other intellectual property right that could be registered such as, but not limited to, the company and its subsidiaries, i.e. the mills, the hubs and others. All subsidiaries and business units are impelled to follow the corporate identity policy and the content of this manual. The goal is to appear as one company with one family of product logos.

- To respect and not infringe other companies’ trademark registrations.
- To apply a strict process for checking availability of new trademarks, designs, slogans etc before launch.
