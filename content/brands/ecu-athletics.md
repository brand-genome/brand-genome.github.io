## Brand Guidelines

### 2019

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The ECU Athletics Brand aims to exemplify the character, passion, spirit, and determination of East Carolina University, representing the university to local, regional, and national audiences with a high-quality image and consistent standards [^1].

##### Core Values

- character
- passion
- spirit
- determination
- discipline
- consistency

##### Target Audience

- Current and former student athletes, students, faculty, staff, alumni, parents, relatives, friends, and broader local, regional, and national audiences [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- proud
- spirited
- determined
- bold
- consistent

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is defined by tightly consistent dimensions, style, symbols, design, character, and colors. Official colors are purple and gold, with accent colors black and white. The primary mark is a stylized pirate skull and crossbones, and Matrix Extra Bold is the designated font. All branding elements are used with strict adherence to guidelines for clarity, integrity, and consistency across all applications [^3].
