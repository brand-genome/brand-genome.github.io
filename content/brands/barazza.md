## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To capture the spirit of who we are through a visual identity that reflects our logo, colours, character, images, and tone of voice, tracing the main characteristics of our brand [^1].

##### Core Values

- Creativity
- Innovation
- Ecosustainability
- Italian skill and mastery
- The value of the people
- The strength of knowledge
- Man and machine
- The culture of materials

##### Target Audience

- Individuals and businesses seeking modern, innovative, and high-quality kitchen design solutions with a focus on Italian craftsmanship and sustainability [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Creative
- Innovative
- Sustainable
- Masterful
- Human-centered
- Knowledge-driven
- Technologically advanced
- Material-focused

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand's visual identity is defined by a clean, minimalist, and modern aesthetic, using a monochrome palette (black, white, steel finishes), clear and bold logo usage, institutional typography (Helvetica Neue), and imagery that emphasizes design, creativity, and inspiration. The logo is always reproduced in black or white on dark backgrounds, with strict guidelines for usage and minimum dimensions. Communication materials and packaging follow consistent layout and color rules, reinforcing a sophisticated and contemporary style [^3].

Our visual identity captures the spirit of who we are and the basic elements of our company: our logo, colours, character, images trace the main characteristics of our brand.
