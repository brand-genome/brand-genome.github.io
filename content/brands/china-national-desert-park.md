## Brand Guidelines

### 2015

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To establish a unified and authoritative visual identity for China National Desert Park, reflecting its role in desert conservation, education, experience, and management, and conveying the spirit and values of the park through visual symbols and design [^1].

##### Core Values

- nature
- mystery
- curiosity
- uniqueness

##### Target Audience

- Visitors, stakeholders, and management of China National Desert Park, including those interested in desert conservation, education, and experience zones [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- authoritative
- unique
- curious
- mysterious
- natural

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a circular emblem inspired by a seal, featuring desert landscapes, camels, birds, and vegetation. The color palette emphasizes browns, blues, and greens to evoke desert environments and vitality. The system includes strict logo usage guidelines, standard and auxiliary colors, recommended fonts, and supplementary graphics to ensure consistency and strong visual impact [^3].
