##### Color Palette

- Using color consistently is one of the easiest ways to build brand recognition. For this reason, it is important when creating Hustler related materials to use only the Hustler color palette provided. Allow our primary brand color to make an impact by surrounding it with our neutral color palette. For example, when displaying a product shot from our line of mowers let the mower become the point of focus by using yellow sparingly elsewhere on the page. Secondary hues of the Hustler yellow may be used but should remain in a supporting role to the primary yellow making up no more than a quarter of the yellow on a page.
