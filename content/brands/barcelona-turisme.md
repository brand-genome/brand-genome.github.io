## Brand Guidelines

### 2016

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To promote Barcelona as a leading tourism destination through professional, visually consistent publications and materials that highlight the city's cultural, architectural, and leisure offerings [^1].

##### Core Values

- professionalism
- clarity
- visual consistency
- collaboration
- cultural richness

##### Target Audience

- Tourists, visitors, and professionals interested in Barcelona's attractions, cultural events, and leisure activities [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- organized
- inviting
- artistic
- dynamic

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses a clean, modern visual style with standardized layouts, prominent logo usage, consistent color palettes (including corporate and product-specific colors), and clear typography. Visual elements such as ovals, rhombuses, and mosaic patterns are used to create distinctive, professional, and vibrant materials. Layouts emphasize clarity, spacing, and the integration of logos, icons, and social media pictograms [^3].

- The visual identity is minimalist and clean, featuring a solid white background, geometric diamond shapes in corporate colors (green, blue, red, yellow), and modern sans-serif typography (FF Meta and Open Sans). The logo and visual elements reference iconic Barcelona symbols, emphasizing clarity, openness, and a contemporary style. Color palette and logo usage are strictly regulated, with guidelines for layout, composition, and print applications [^3].

#### Categories

##### Brand Imagery

- Minimalist White Background - The image should feature a clean, minimalistic design with a solid white background and no additional elements, text, or graphics, emphasizing simplicity and openness.
- Colorful Diamonds in a Row - The image should depict three solid-colored diamonds arranged in a horizontal row with equal spacing between them against a plain white background. The leftmost diamond is green, the middle diamond is blue, and the rightmost diamond is red. Each diamond is oriented so that one vertex points directly upward, and the opposite vertex points downward, forming perfect rhombuses with equal side lengths. The diamonds are of the same size and shape, aligned at the same angle, and evenly spaced to create a balanced, symmetrical composition. The background is uniformly white, emphasizing the vivid colors of the diamonds.
- Barcelona Tourism Logo Design - The image should feature a clean, rectangular layout with a white background and a thin light gray border. In the top left corner, the text '11a x 5a' appears in small, black font. Below that, aligned to the left, there are four small, solid-colored diamonds arranged in a slight arc or line, each positioned above the preceding text: a green diamond on the far left, followed by a blue diamond, a red diamond, and a yellow diamond on the far right. The diamonds are uniform in size and neatly spaced. Centered prominently in the middle of the image, the word 'Barcelona' is displayed in large, bold, black font, using a sans-serif typeface. Directly beneath it, the word 'Turisme' appears in a slightly smaller, lighter, and italicized font, also black. The overall composition balances the geometric shapes above with the textual elements below, maintaining alignment and clear spacing.
