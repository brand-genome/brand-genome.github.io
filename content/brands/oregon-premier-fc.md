## Brand Guidelines

### 2019

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Oregon Premier Football Club will provide the highest possible level of technical and tactical soccer training, athletic development and competitive team play. We will help our players be the best soccer players, students and people they can be.

##### Core Values

- piety
- sincerity
- honor
- loyalty
- regional pride
- partnership
- community

##### Target Audience

- Soccer players, students, and people seeking athletic development and competitive team play in Oregon, including those from Lake Oswego and Willamette United Soccer Clubs.

##### Personality Traits

- professional
- competitive
- honorable
- loyal
- community-focused
- sincere

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on shield-shaped emblems, the silhouette of Oregon, bold typography, and a color palette of navy blue, gold, gray, and white. The design emphasizes regional pride, partnership, and consistency, with heraldic and sports motifs throughout logos and uniforms.
