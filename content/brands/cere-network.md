## Brand Guidelines

### 2022

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- Truly own your data for the first time. Cere Network is the first Decentralized Data Cloud (DDC) platform built on Substrate and Polygon, facilitating trustless cloud data storage, integration, and data collaboration across blockchain networks, as well as Web3 NFT, Metaverse, and gaming content.

##### Core Values

- decentralization
- trustless collaboration
- data ownership
- innovation

##### Target Audience

- Organizations and individuals seeking secure, decentralized cloud data storage and integration, especially in Web3, NFT, Metaverse, and gaming sectors.

##### Personality Traits

- innovative
- modern
- tech-focused
- collaborative

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The brand uses a modern, abstract logo inspired by the cerebellum, with a gradient color palette (purple, pink, cyan, navy), clean sans-serif typography (Sora and Poppins), and a visual style emphasizing minimalism, clarity, and technological themes. Illustrations are abstract and modern, suitable for digital platforms.
