#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To provide a unified and consistent corporate identity for Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality, ensuring integrity and continuity across all communication and design applications [^1].

##### Core Values

- unity
- integrity
- continuity
- professionalism

##### Target Audience

- Employees, affiliates, and stakeholders of Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality, as well as the general public interacting with municipal communications [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- modern
- authoritative
- clean
- minimalistic

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is characterized by a modern, clean, and minimalistic style, with a strong emphasis on blue tones, geometric and architectural motifs, and clear sans-serif typography. The logo is central to the brand, with strict rules on its usage, proportions, and color variations. Layouts are precise, balanced, and professional, reflecting civic strength and cultural heritage [^3].
