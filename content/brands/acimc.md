## Brand Guidelines

### 2014

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To represent and promote Iranian contemporary music composers, blending Persian cultural motifs with a contemporary visual identity [^1].

##### Core Values

- Cultural heritage
- Contemporary expression
- Elegance
- Simplicity
- Professionalism

##### Target Audience

- Composers, musicians, and audiences interested in contemporary Iranian music, as well as cultural and artistic communities [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- Elegant
- Minimalist
- Refined
- Contemporary
- Classic

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on color and geometric patterns, references to Persian calligraphy and architecture, a turquoise diamond motif, minimalist line art, and a modern serif logotype. The logo and logotype are used separately, with strict layout and color guidelines. The palette includes turquoise, gold, and monochrome variants, and typography is based on Adobe Caslon Pro with small caps for titles [^3].
