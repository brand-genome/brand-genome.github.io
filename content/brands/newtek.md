## Brand Guidelines

### 2019

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- NewTek gives storytellers a voice through video. We use innovation and technology to fundamentally change the way video is created and used, allowing more people to share their stories, experiences, and ideas with the world and enjoy doing it. The creativity and ingenuity of our employees are the key to our success, and we genuinely care about them and their families [^1].

##### Core Values

- innovation
- creativity
- technology-driven
- accessibility
- care for employees and families

##### Target Audience

- Storytellers, broadcasters, content creators, organizations ranging from sports teams, news agencies, entertainment companies, educational institutions, and corporate communicators who want to capture and publish live video [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- innovative
- fun
- affordable
- easy to use
- professional
- high-tech

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity features crisp product renders, emotional product photography, and environmental images. The brand uses white, light grey, and black backgrounds punctuated by bright colors, surrounded by ample white or negative space. Helvetica Neue is the primary font, with Arial as an alternative. The logo is used with strict clear space and size guidelines, and the color palette includes NewTek Blue, Black, Grey, White, Light Grey, and Orange [^3].

#### Categories

##### Brand Imagery

- Photography is a key element of the NewTek brand look.
- Our images fall under one of three specific style applications: Product Images, Environmental Images, and "Emotional" Product Images.
- Product photos, or 3D renders, include front views and 3/4 angles of each product.
- Photos that show the system in use are used largely for promotional materials. Visuals typically show customers using our systems.
- Visuals are close-up shots of product elements, such as control surface buttons, switcher inputs, and other details. Focus and angles vary by product. Color is highly saturated and brightly lit on a dark background.

##### Logo Usage

- BLUE indicates CLEAR SPACE.
- The BLUE area must be kept free of other elements. Grey padding indicates SAFE ZONE.
- MAGENTA indicates type and element alignment boundaries.
- The minimum required CLEAR SPACE is defined by the measurement 'X' (Equal to the height of the uppercase letters, known as the 'cap-height').
- Use the color NewTek logo, whenever possible, in all worldwide marketing and advertising materials.
- Minimum Logo width -35mm 35mm is the safest minimum size
- It is extremely important for brand success that the logotype be displayed correctly. This includes always using the correct typefaces, relative positioning, and size of all elements. The examples in this section illustrate some unacceptable displays.
- Do not distort any portion of the logotype.
- Do not crop any portion of the logotype.
- Do not tilt the logotype.
- Do not rotate the logotype.
- (An obvious exception to this guideline would be a vertical street pole banner with logo appearing as the primary element in a vertical position by necessity.)
- Do not rearrange components in the signature.
- Do not alter the alignment of any component of the signature.
- Do not alter the typeface of the logotype.
- Do not alter the type style (e.g., from all caps to cap/lowercase) of the signature.
- Do not add a drop shadow to any part of the signature.
- Do not alter the relative size of the logotype and logomark.
