## Brand Guidelines

### 2018

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The document does not explicitly state a mission or purpose statement for the Generalitat Valenciana. Its focus is on the visual and corporate identity standards for official communication and representation [^1].

##### Core Values

- officiality
- coherence
- regional identity
- clarity
- authority

##### Target Audience

- Government departments, dependent and independent entities, and designers responsible for applying the Generalitat Valenciana's brand identity in official documents, signage, vehicles, uniforms, and digital environments [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- formal
- authoritative
- institutional
- minimalist
- coherent

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is centered on a heraldic emblem featuring a stylized dragon, crown, shield, cross, and red stripes, with strict rules for logo construction, color palette (Pantone 186 C red and black), minimum sizes, clear areas of respect, and consistent use of the Roboto typeface. The style is minimalist, official, and highly structured, with guidelines for application across stationery, signage, vehicles, uniforms, and digital assets [^3].
