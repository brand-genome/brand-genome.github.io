## Brand Guidelines

### 2020

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- The Nintendo Switch brand guide does not explicitly state a mission or purpose statement, but the visual and messaging focus is on providing a modern, versatile gaming experience that emphasizes play anywhere, social interaction, and accessibility [^1].

##### Core Values

- innovation
- versatility
- accessibility
- social play
- quality

##### Target Audience

- The target audience includes families, children, young adults, and gamers seeking flexible, portable, and social gaming experiences [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- playful
- modern
- energetic
- inclusive
- friendly

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is characterized by bold red and white color palette, clean and modern logo featuring Joy-Con motifs, minimalistic layouts, and vibrant imagery that highlights the console's versatility and social play [^3].

#### Categories

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity centers on a clean, minimalistic design with bold, geometric iconography inspired by the Joy-Con controllers. The primary color palette features vivid Nintendo Red, white, black, and gray, with strong emphasis on logo clarity, proportion, and clear space.
