##### Visual Identity Overview

- The visual identity is lively, friendly, and fun, with a cartoon-style, bright color palette, playful typography, consistent illustration style, and iconic graphic elements like cows, clouds, and rolling hills. The logo is a cornerstone, always used with strict guidelines, and benefit logos are presented on wooden signs. The brand emphasizes consistency, recognizability, and a whimsical, approachable style [^3].

##### Tone And Messaging

- Our Voice is… Genuine, Personable, Informal, Inclusive, Offbeat, Audacious, Conversational, balanced & direct, factual or contrived, moral, off-putting, obnoxious, chatty, elicit. NOT pretentious or pompous, NOT elitist, NOT corporate, NOT fake or bureaucratic, NOT obnoxious.
- Our Language and Humor Is... Clever & Quirky NOT corny (less corn, more wry)
- slightly sassy NEVER snide, snarky or sarcastic
- witty, wacky NOT slick or slapstick
- playful, fun NOT cutsey, cliché, childish or cartoony
- We're not talking to kids; we're talking to adults (and their inner kids).
- We want to reach the kid inside every adult so we use descriptive words that evoke a childlike playfulness (ooey-gooey, chocolatey-chunky).
- At the same time, we don't want to alienate or talk down to the adult, so we avoid using words that are overly cutesy or juvenile (yummy, tummy, belly).
- Wordplay is Fun... ...When it's not overdone
- We'd rather invent an unconventional catch-phrase (Euphoria Aheadia) than be overdependent on a conventional catch-phrase (Here's The Scoop).
- Watch out for worn-out words, overdone puns and cliches: • corny compound words ending in "-tastic" or "-licious" • dueling "moo"-isms and "cow"-isms • barnyard banter gone bonkers • heifers and hooves and "udder" absurdity in every other sentence
- Don't follow the herd Do be outstanding in the field
- Dicey double entendres, suggestive expressions, etc. We're not prudes about such usage but it's best to be double-extra judicious about it.
- Bottom line: we think it's better to be strategically cheeky than to end up being the butt of jokes.
- Be sure to check not only for goofs and typos but also for any telltale lapses in voice or loss of alignment with our personality.
- Additionally, checkout our list of brand-specific words with spelling consistency rules.
- "tastebuds" not "taste buds" (while both are correct, we prefer the 1-word version) "Scoop Shop" not "scoop shap" or "scoopshop" "Chocolatey" not "chocolaty" "PartnerShop®" not "partnershop" or "partner shop" -and not without the® "super premium" not "superpremium," or "super-premium," or "Super Premium."
- We're not the punctuation police, but if we had to pick one thing that really really bugs us, it'd be excessive use of exclamation marks. we think they're best when used to catch the eye, not! poke! it! out!
