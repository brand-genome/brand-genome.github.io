## Brand Guidelines

### 2024

#### Brand Summary

##### Mission

- To provide clear standards for representing the Misfits brand both internally and externally, ensuring a professional and approachable identity [^1].

##### Core Values

- approachability
- friendliness
- playfulness
- clarity
- consistency

##### Target Audience

- A broad audience, including youth and community groups, with a focus on casual, fun, and inclusive branding [^2].

##### Personality Traits

- friendly
- playful
- approachable
- informal
- energetic

##### Visual Identity Overview

- The Misfits brand uses a cartoon-style mascot and logo, bold blue as the main color, playful rounded typography, and vibrant color palettes. Visuals emphasize clean layouts, safe space around logos, and energetic elements like stripes and expressive mascot faces [^3].
